# Own Page - Accounts

Explore the **Accounts** tab in **Own Page** to view and manage connected accounts. The **Groups** page follows the same general functionality and flow.

This workflow is intended for users who want to review accounts that posted news and content about the brand and inspect their related performance details.

---

## Step 1: Open the Analytics page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Analytics icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-accounts/own-page-accounts_step1.png)

Tap the **Analytics** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the Analytics page.

---

## Step 2: Open the Accounts tab

![Placeholder screenshot for the Accounts tab in Own Page](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-accounts/own-page-accounts_step2.png)

Tap the **Accounts** tab in **Own Page** to view the accounts that posted news and content about the brand.

---

## Step 3: Open the filter

![Placeholder screenshot for the filter button in the accounts view](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-accounts/own-page-accounts_step3.png)

Tap the filter button to filter accounts based on posts and followers data.

---

## Step 4: Apply the filter

![Placeholder screenshot for the Apply button in account filters](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-accounts/own-page-accounts_step4.png)

Tap **Apply** to update the accounts list based on the selected filters.

---

## Step 5: Open AI Copilot

![Placeholder screenshot for the AI Copilot button in the accounts view](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-accounts/own-page-accounts_step5.png)

Tap the AI Copilot button to ask questions and get insights about the accounts data.

---

## Step 6: Enter a question

![Placeholder screenshot for the Copilot input box in the accounts view](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-accounts/own-page-accounts_step6.png)

Write any question in AI Copilot to get answers and insights based on the accounts data.

---

## Step 7: View profile details

![Placeholder screenshot for the View Profile Details action](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-accounts/own-page-accounts_step7.png)

Tap **View Profile Details** for any account to view its charts and related posts.

---

## Step 8: View the profile details page

![Placeholder screenshot for the account profile details page](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-accounts/own-page-accounts_step8.png)

View the account performance charts and related posts from the profile details page.
