# Own Page - Analytics

Explore the **Analytics** tab in **Own Page** to view performance metrics, charts, and insights about your page data. The **Groups** page follows the same general functionality and flow.

This workflow is intended for users who want to understand the performance of their page and explore related analytics insights.

---

## Step 1: Open the Analytics page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Analytics icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-analytics/own-page-analytics_step1.png)

Tap the **Analytics** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the Analytics page.

---

## Step 2: Open the Own Page tab

![Placeholder screenshot for the Own Page tab](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-analytics/own-page-analytics_step2.png)

Tap the **Own Page** tab in the top navigation bar to access your page analytics and insights.

---

## Step 3: Open the Analytics tab

![Placeholder screenshot for the Analytics tab in Own Page](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-analytics/own-page-analytics_step3.png)

Tap the **Analytics** tab in **Own Page** to view performance metrics, charts, and insights.

---

## Step 4: View the Analytics page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Own Page analytics view](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-analytics/own-page-analytics_step4.png)

View analytics data, performance metrics, and charts for the selected page.

---

## Step 5: Open AI Copilot

![Placeholder screenshot for the AI Copilot button above the chart](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-analytics/own-page-analytics_step5.png)

Tap the AI Copilot button above the chart to ask questions and get insights about the analytics data.

---

## Step 6: Enter a question

![Placeholder screenshot for the Copilot input box](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/own-page/own-page-analytics/own-page-analytics_step6.png)

Write any question in AI Copilot to get answers and insights based on the chart data.
