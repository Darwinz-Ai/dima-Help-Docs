# Open Notification Page

Viewing the Notifications page allows users to access all notifications and stay informed about important updates and activities within the Dima application. Users simply open the Notifications page to review their recent and previous notifications.

This workflow is intended for users who want to stay up to date with application events and alerts.

---

## Step 1: Open the navigation menu

![Placeholder screenshot for the highlighted header button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/open-notification-page/open-notification-page_step1.png)

Tap the highlighted button in the top-left corner of the header to open the navigation menu.

---

## Step 2: Open the Notifications page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Notifications option in the menu](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/open-notification-page/open-notification-page_step2.png)

Tap the **Notifications** option in the navigation menu to open the Notifications page.

---

## Step 3: View notification details

![Placeholder screenshot for the notification list and details](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/open-notification-page/open-notification-page_step3.png)

Tap any notification on the Notifications page to open it and view its details.

---

## Step 4: Open the AI Copilot assistant

![Placeholder screenshot for the AI Copilot icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/open-notification-page/open-notification-page_step4.png)

Tap the AI Copilot icon in the top-left corner of the header to open the AI assistant and ask questions about the selected notification.

---

## Step 5: Ask a question about the notification

![Placeholder screenshot for the chat input field in AI Copilot](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/open-notification-page/open-notification-page_step5.png)

Type your question in the chat input field, then tap the **Send** button to receive an AI-generated response based on the selected notification.

---

## Step 6: Export the notification

![Placeholder screenshot for the export button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/open-notification-page/open-notification-page_step6.png)

Tap the export button in the top-right corner of the header to export the selected notification.

---

## Step 7: Select an export destination

![Placeholder screenshot for the export destination options](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/open-notification-page/open-notification-page_step7.png)

When the export options screen appears, select your preferred destination to export the notification.
