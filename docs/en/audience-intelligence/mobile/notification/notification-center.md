# Notification Center

Managing the Notification Center allows users to control which types of notifications they receive from the Dima application. Users simply open the Notification Center, select their preferred notification settings, and save the changes.

This workflow is intended for users who want to customize their notification preferences.

---

## Step 1: Open the navigation menu

![Placeholder screenshot for the highlighted header button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/notification-center/notification-center_step1.png)

Tap the highlighted button in the top-left corner of the header to open the navigation menu.

---

## Step 2: Open the Notification Center

![Placeholder screenshot for the Notification Center option in the menu](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/notification-center/notification-center_step2.png)

Tap the **Notification Center** option in the navigation menu to open the Notification Center page.

---

## Step 3: Manage your notification preferences

![Placeholder screenshot for notification subscription toggles](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/notification-center/notification-center_step3.png)

Enable or disable the notification categories by using the toggle buttons next to each subscription.

---

## Step 4: Review your selected subscriptions

![Placeholder screenshot for updated notification preferences](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/notification-center/notification-center_step3.png)

Adjust the notification categories again if needed until your preferred subscription settings are selected.

---

## Step 5: Save your preferences

![Placeholder screenshot for the Save button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/notification-center/notification-center_step5.png)

Tap the **Save** button to apply your notification subscription preferences.

---

## Step 6: Verify the subscriptions were saved

![Placeholder screenshot for the success confirmation message](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/notification/notification-center/notification-center_step6.png)

Verify that a confirmation message appears indicating that your notification subscription preferences have been saved successfully.
