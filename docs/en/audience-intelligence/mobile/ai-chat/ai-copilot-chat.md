# AI Copilot Chat

Using the AI Copilot chat allows users to interact with the AI assistant to ask questions, receive guidance, and get instant responses. Users simply open the AI Copilot, type their message, and submit it to receive assistance.

This workflow is intended for users who need information, support, or AI-powered recommendations within the Dima application.

---

## Step 1: Open the AI Copilot chat

![Placeholder screenshot for the Let's Chat button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/ai-chat/ai-copilot-chat/ai-copilot-chat_step1.png)

Tap the **Let's Chat** button to open the AI Copilot chat screen and start a new conversation.

---

## Step 2: Enter your question or message

![Placeholder screenshot for the chat input field](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/ai-chat/ai-copilot-chat/ai-copilot-chat_step2.png)

Type your question or message in the chat input field to start a conversation with the AI Copilot.

---

## Step 3: Open the recommended questions

![Placeholder screenshot for the recommended questions button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/ai-chat/ai-copilot-chat/ai-copilot-chat_step3.png)

Tap the leftmost button in the chat screen to open the list of recommended questions.
