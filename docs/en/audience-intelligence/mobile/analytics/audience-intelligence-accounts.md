# Audience Intelligence Accounts

Audience Intelligence Accounts allows users to explore the social media accounts associated with a selected brand within the Dima application. Users simply open the Accounts tab to view account details, monitor account activity, and access related information.

This workflow is intended for users who want to analyze and manage the brand's social media presence.

---

## Step 1: Open the Analytics page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Analytics icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-accounts/audience-intelligence-accounts_step1.png)

Tap the **Analytics** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the Analytics page.

---

## Step 2: Open the Accounts tab

![Placeholder screenshot for the Accounts tab](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-accounts/audience-intelligence-accounts_step2.png)

Tap the **Accounts** tab in the top navigation bar to open the Audience Intelligence Accounts page.

---

## Step 3: View brand accounts

![Placeholder screenshot for the list of brand accounts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-accounts/audience-intelligence-accounts_step3.png)

View all social media accounts associated with the selected brand.

---

## Step 4: Open the account filters

![Placeholder screenshot for the Filter icon and account filters](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-accounts/audience-intelligence-accounts_step4.png)

Tap the **Filter** icon to open the available account filters and refine the displayed accounts based on criteria such as followers, posts, and other available options.

---

## Step 5: Apply the selected filters

![Placeholder screenshot for the Apply button in account filters](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-accounts/audience-intelligence-accounts_step5.png)

Tap the **Apply** button to apply the selected filter criteria and update the displayed accounts.

---

## Step 6: Open an account

![Placeholder screenshot for opening an account from the list](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-accounts/audience-intelligence-accounts_step6.png)

Open an account to view its profile details and additional account information.

---

## Step 7: View account details

![Placeholder screenshot for the account profile details](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-accounts/audience-intelligence-accounts_step7.png)

View the account profile, including analytics, performance charts, and recent posts.
