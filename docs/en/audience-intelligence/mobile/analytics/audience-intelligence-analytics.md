# Audience Intelligence Analytics

Audience Intelligence Analytics allows users to explore insights and trends about their audience within the Dima application. Users simply open the Audience Intelligence Analytics page to view key metrics, demographic data, and audience behavior.

This workflow is intended for users who want to better understand their audience and make data-driven decisions.

---

## Step 1: Open the Analytics page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Analytics icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-analytics/audience-intelligence-analytics_step1.png)

Tap the **Analytics** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the Analytics page.

---

## Step 2: View analytics charts

![Placeholder screenshot for the analytics charts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-analytics/audience-intelligence-analytics_step2.png)

View the analytics charts to explore key audience insights and performance metrics.

---

## Step 3: Open AI Copilot

![Placeholder screenshot for the AI Copilot icon above the chart](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-analytics/audience-intelligence-analytics_step3.png)

Tap the AI Copilot icon above the chart to open AI Copilot and ask questions about the selected chart.

---

## Step 4: Enter your question

![Placeholder screenshot for the AI Copilot chat box](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-analytics/audience-intelligence-analytics_step4.png)

Type your question in the AI Copilot chat box to receive insights about the selected chart.
