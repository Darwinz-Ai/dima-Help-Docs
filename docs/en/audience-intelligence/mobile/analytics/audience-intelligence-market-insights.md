# Audience Intelligence Market Insights

Audience Intelligence Market Insights allows users to compare their brand's performance with competitors within the Dima application. Users simply open the Market Insights tab to view comparative analytics, key performance metrics, and market trends.

This workflow is intended for users who want to benchmark their brand against competitors and gain valuable market insights.

---

## Step 1: Open the Analytics page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Analytics icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-market-insights/audience-intelligence-market-insights_step1.png)

Tap the **Analytics** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the Analytics page.

---

## Step 2: Open the Market Insights tab

![Placeholder screenshot for the Market Insights tab](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-market-insights/audience-intelligence-market-insights_step2.png)

Tap the **Market Insights** tab to open the Market Insights page and explore market trends, topics, and competitor insights.

---

## Step 3: View the Market Insights page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Market Insights page](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-market-insights/audience-intelligence-market-insights_step3.png)

Explore market insights and analytics data on the Market Insights page.

---

## Step 4: Open AI Copilot

![Placeholder screenshot for the AI Copilot button above the chart](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-market-insights/audience-intelligence-market-insights_step4.png)

Tap the AI Copilot button above the chart to ask questions and get insights about the chart data.

---

## Step 5: Enter a question

![Placeholder screenshot for the Copilot question input](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-market-insights/audience-intelligence-market-insights_step5.png)

Enter any question in AI Copilot to get insights and answers based on the chart data.

---

## Step 6: Open the filter

![Placeholder screenshot for the filter button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-market-insights/audience-intelligence-market-insights_step7.png)

Tap the filter button to select topics and competitors to analyze and compare their insights.

---

## Step 7: Select filter options

![Placeholder screenshot for the topics and competitors filter options](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-market-insights/audience-intelligence-market-insights_step8.png)

Select the topics and competitors from the available filters, then tap **Apply** to update the chart insights.
