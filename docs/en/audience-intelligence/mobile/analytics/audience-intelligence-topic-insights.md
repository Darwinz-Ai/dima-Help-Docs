# Audience Intelligence Topic Insights

Audience Intelligence Topic Insights allows users to explore insights and trends related to specific topics within the Dima application. Users simply open the Topic Insights tab to view topic analytics, related content, and performance metrics.

This workflow is intended for users who want to analyze topic trends and better understand audience interests.

---

## Step 1: Open the Topic Insights tab

![Placeholder screenshot for the Topic Insights tab](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-topic-insights/audience-intelligence-topic-insights_step1.png)

Tap the **Topic Insights** tab to open the Audience Intelligence Topic Insights page.

---

## Step 2: View topic insights

![Placeholder screenshot for topic insights and analytics](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-topic-insights/audience-intelligence-topic-insights_step2.png)

View topic insights, including analytics, performance metrics, and related content.

---

## Step 3: Open the topic filter

![Placeholder screenshot for the topic filter or selector](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-topic-insights/audience-intelligence-topic-insights_step3.png)

Tap the **Filter** icon to open the topic selector and choose a different topic to view its insights.

---

## Step 4: Apply the selected topic

![Placeholder screenshot for the Apply button in topic selection](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-topic-insights/audience-intelligence-topic-insights_step4.png)

Tap the **Apply** button to apply the selected topic and update the displayed insights.
