# Audience Intelligence Monitor

Audience Intelligence Monitor allows users to review posts and mentions related to the selected brand inside the Dima application. Users can open the monitor view, follow audience conversations, and inspect post images more closely when needed.

This workflow is intended for users who want to monitor audience conversations and engagement around a selected brand.

---

## Step 1: Open the Analytics page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Analytics icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-monitor/audience-intelligence-monitor_step1.png)

Tap the **Analytics** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the Analytics page.

---

## Step 2: Open the Monitor tab

![Placeholder screenshot for the Monitor tab](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-monitor/audience-intelligence-monitor_step2.png)

Tap the **Monitor** tab in the top navigation bar to open the Audience Intelligence Monitor page.

---

## Step 3: View brand mentions

![Placeholder screenshot for brand mentions and posts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-monitor/audience-intelligence-monitor_step3.png)

View posts and mentions related to the selected brand to monitor audience conversations and engagement.

---

## Step 4: Open the post image

![Placeholder screenshot for opening a post image](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-monitor/audience-intelligence-monitor_step4.png)

Tap the post image to open it in full-screen view.

---

## Step 5: View the post image

![Placeholder screenshot for the full-screen post image](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/analytics/audience-intelligence-monitor/audience-intelligence-monitor_step5.png)

View the selected post image in a larger format for better visibility.
