# Open Onboarding Screens

Opening the onboarding screens allows users to view the introduction to the Dima application before accessing its main features. Users simply launch the app and move through each onboarding page until they reach the next stage of the application.

This workflow is intended for first-time users or for users opening the application again after reinstalling it.

---

## Step 1: Tap the button in the top-right corner of the header

![Placeholder screenshot for the onboarding entry button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/open-onboarding-screens/open-onboarding-screens_step1.png)

Tap the button in the top-right corner of the header to begin navigating through the onboarding experience.

---

## Step 2: Go to the next onboarding screen

![Placeholder screenshot for the first Next button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/open-onboarding-screens/open-onboarding-screens_step2.png)

Tap the **Next** button in the bottom-right corner of the onboarding screen to continue.

---

## Step 3: Continue to the following onboarding screen

![Placeholder screenshot for the second Next button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/open-onboarding-screens/open-onboarding-screens_step3.png)

Tap the **Next** button again to move to the next onboarding screen.

---

## Step 4: Complete the onboarding flow

![Placeholder screenshot for the Let's Go button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/open-onboarding-screens/open-onboarding-screens_step4.png)

Tap the **Let's Go** button at the bottom of the onboarding screen to complete the onboarding process.
