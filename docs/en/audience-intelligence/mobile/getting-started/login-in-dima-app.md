# Login in Dima App

Logging into the Dima application allows users to securely access their accounts and personalized features. Users simply launch the app, complete the onboarding flow if needed, enter their credentials, and sign in to continue to the home page.

This workflow is intended for registered users accessing the Audience Intelligence mobile application for the first time or returning after reinstalling the app.

---

## Step 1: Open the Dima application

![Placeholder screenshot for opening the Dima application](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/login-in-dima-app/login-in-dima-app_step1.png)

Launch the Dima mobile application on your device to begin the login process.

---

## Step 2: Select your preferred language

![Placeholder screenshot for choosing the preferred language](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/login-in-dima-app/login-in-dima-app_step2.png)

Choose your preferred language by tapping the corresponding button at the bottom of the screen.

---

## Step 3: Continue or skip the first onboarding screen

![Placeholder screenshot for the first onboarding screen](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/login-in-dima-app/login-in-dima-app_step3.png)

Tap **Next** to continue through the onboarding screens, or tap **Skip** to proceed directly to the login screen.

---

## Step 4: Continue or skip the next onboarding screen

![Placeholder screenshot for the second onboarding screen](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/login-in-dima-app/login-in-dima-app_step4.png)

If another onboarding screen appears, tap **Next** to continue or **Skip** to move directly to the login screen.

---

## Step 5: Complete onboarding

![Placeholder screenshot for the final onboarding screen](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/login-in-dima-app/login-in-dima-app_step5.png)

Tap the **Let's Go** button at the bottom of the onboarding screen to complete onboarding and proceed to the login screen.

---

## Step 6: Choose whether to enable notifications

![Placeholder screenshot for the notifications prompt](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/login-in-dima-app/login-in-dima-app_step6.png)

Tap **Enable Notifications** to allow the app to send alerts and updates, or tap **Not Now** to continue without enabling notifications.

---

## Step 7: Confirm the system notification permission

![Placeholder screenshot for the system permission dialog](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/login-in-dima-app/login-in-dima-app_step7.png)

If the system permission dialog appears, tap **Allow** to enable notifications for the Dima application.

---

## Step 8: Enter your login credentials

![Placeholder screenshot for the login form](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/login-in-dima-app/login-in-dima-app_step8.png)

Enter your registered email address or username and password, then tap the **Login** button to sign in.

---

## Step 9: Wait for verification

![Placeholder screenshot for the loading or verification state](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/getting-started/login-in-dima-app/login-in-dima-app_step9.png)

Wait while the application verifies your login credentials. Once verification is complete, you will be taken to the home page.
