# Google Reviews

Viewing Google Reviews allows users to access customer feedback for a selected brand within the Dima application. Users simply open the brand, navigate to the Google Reviews section, and review the available ratings and comments.

This workflow is intended for users who want to monitor customer sentiment and gain insights into a brand's reputation.

---

## Step 1: Open the Analytics page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Analytics icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/google-reviews/google-reviews/google-reviews_step1.png)

Tap the **Analytics** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the Analytics page.

---

## Step 2: Open the Google Reviews tab

![Placeholder screenshot for the Google Reviews tab](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/google-reviews/google-reviews/google-reviews_step2.png)

Tap the **Google Reviews** tab in the top navigation bar to open the Google Reviews page.

---

## Step 3: Open the Analyze tab

![Placeholder screenshot for the Analyze tab](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/google-reviews/google-reviews/google-reviews_step3.png)

Tap the **Analyze** tab at the top of the Google Reviews page to view analytics and insights for the selected brand's reviews.

---

## Step 4: View the review analytics

![Placeholder screenshot for Google Reviews analytics](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/google-reviews/google-reviews/google-reviews_step4.png)

Review the available analytics, including review trends, sentiment insights, and key performance metrics for the selected brand.

---

## Step 5: Open the filters

![Placeholder screenshot for the Filter icon in Google Reviews](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/google-reviews/google-reviews/google-reviews_step5.png)

Tap the **Filter** icon to open the available filters for the review analytics.

---

## Step 6: View branch details

![Placeholder screenshot for a branch details entry](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/google-reviews/google-reviews/google-reviews_step6.png)

Tap the desired branch to open its details page and view the associated Google Reviews.

---

## Step 7: View the Google Reviews

![Placeholder screenshot for branch-level Google Reviews](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/google-reviews/google-reviews/google-reviews_step7.png)

Review the selected branch's Google Reviews, including ratings, review distribution, and customer feedback.
