# Change The Application Theme

Changing the application theme allows users to personalize the application's appearance by selecting their preferred theme. Users simply open the Theme settings, choose the desired theme, and save their changes.

This workflow is intended for users who want to customize the application's visual appearance according to their preferences.

---

## Step 1: Open the navigation menu

![Placeholder screenshot for the highlighted header button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/change-the-application-theme/change-the-application-theme_step1.png)

Tap the highlighted button in the top-left corner of the header to open the navigation menu.

---

## Step 2: Open the Settings screen

![Placeholder screenshot for the Settings option in the menu](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/change-the-application-theme/change-the-application-theme_step2.png)

Scroll to the bottom of the navigation menu and tap **Settings** to open the application settings.

---

## Step 3: Tap the Theme toggle

![Placeholder screenshot for the Theme option in Settings](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/change-the-application-theme/change-the-application-theme_step3.png)

The **Theme** option appears in the middle of the **Settings** screen. Tap the toggle button next to it to switch the application theme.

---

## Step 4: Confirm the theme change

![Placeholder screenshot for the updated theme state](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/change-the-application-theme/change-the-application-theme_step4.png)

Tap the **Theme** toggle again if needed until the preferred theme is selected, then verify that the application reflects the updated appearance.
