# Sign Out

Signing out of the Dima application allows users to securely end their current session. Users simply open the navigation menu, access the settings, and select the Sign Out option to log out of the application.

This workflow is intended for users who want to securely exit their accounts.

---

## Step 1: Open the navigation menu

![Placeholder screenshot for the highlighted header button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/sign-out/sign-out_step1.png)

Tap the highlighted button in the top-left corner of the header to open the navigation menu.

---

## Step 2: Open the Settings screen

![Placeholder screenshot for the Settings option in the menu](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/sign-out/sign-out_step2.png)

Scroll to the bottom of the navigation menu and tap **Settings** to open the application settings.

---

## Step 3: Tap the Sign Out button

![Placeholder screenshot for the Sign Out button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/sign-out/sign-out_step3.png)

Tap the **Sign Out** button to securely log out of the Dima application and end your current session.
