# Delete An Account

Deleting an account allows users to permanently remove their Dima account and all associated data. Users simply navigate to the account settings, select the Delete Account option, and confirm their decision.

This workflow is intended for users who no longer wish to use the application and understand that this action cannot be undone.

---

## Step 1: Open the navigation menu

![Placeholder screenshot for the highlighted header button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/delete-an-account/delete-an-account_step1.png)

Tap the highlighted button in the top-left corner of the header to open the navigation menu.

---

## Step 2: Open the Settings screen

![Placeholder screenshot for the Settings option in the menu](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/delete-an-account/delete-an-account_step2.png)

Scroll to the bottom of the navigation menu and tap **Settings** to open the application settings.

---

## Step 3: Start the delete account process

![Placeholder screenshot for the Delete Account button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/delete-an-account/delete-an-account_step3.png)

Tap the **Delete Account** button to begin the account deletion process.

---

## Step 4: Confirm account deletion

![Placeholder screenshot for the delete account confirmation dialog](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/delete-an-account/delete-an-account_step3.png)

A confirmation dialog will appear asking whether you want to permanently delete your account. Tap **Confirm** to proceed with the deletion, or tap **Cancel** to keep your account.
