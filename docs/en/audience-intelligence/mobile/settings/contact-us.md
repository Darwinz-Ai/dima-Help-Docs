# Contact Us

The Contact Us feature allows users to get in touch with the Dima support team for inquiries, feedback, or assistance. Users simply open the Contact Us page, enter the required information, write their message, and submit it.

This workflow is intended for users who need support or want to communicate with the Dima team.

---

## Step 1: Open the navigation menu

![Placeholder screenshot for the highlighted header button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/contact-us/contact-us_step1.png)

Tap the highlighted button in the top-left corner of the header to open the navigation menu.

---

## Step 2: Open the Contact Us page

![Placeholder screenshot for the Contact Us option in the menu](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/contact-us/contact-us_step2.png)

Tap the **Contact Us** option in the navigation menu to open the Contact Us page.

---

## Step 3: Enter your message

![Placeholder screenshot for the message field](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/contact-us/contact-us_step3.png)

Type your message in the message field. Include the details you want to share with the Dima support team.

---

## Step 4: Submit your message

![Placeholder screenshot for the Submit button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/contact-us/contact-us_step4.png)

Tap the **Submit** button to send your message to the Dima support team.

---

## Step 5: Confirm successful submission

![Placeholder screenshot for the success confirmation message](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/settings/contact-us/contact-us_step5.png)

Verify that a confirmation message appears indicating that your message has been submitted successfully.
