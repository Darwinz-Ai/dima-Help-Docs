# Change Brand

Changing the selected brand allows users to customize the content and notifications they receive within the Dima application. Users simply open the brand selection page, choose their preferred brand, and save the changes.

This workflow is intended for users who want to receive data and notifications related to a specific brand.

---

## Step 1: Open the Brands selector

![Placeholder screenshot for the Brands button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/brand/change-brand/change-brand_step1.png)

Tap the **Brands** button on the right side of the bottom navigation bar to open the brand selection page.

---

## Step 2: Select a brand

![Placeholder screenshot for the brands bottom sheet](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/brand/change-brand/change-brand_step2.png)

A bottom sheet will appear displaying the available brands. Tap your preferred brand to select it.

---

## Step 3: Save the selected brand

![Placeholder screenshot for the Save button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/brand/change-brand/change-brand_step3.png)

Tap the **Save** button to apply your selected brand.

---

## Step 4: Verify the brand was changed

![Placeholder screenshot for the updated brand state](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/ar/audience-intelligence/mobile/brand/change-brand/change-brand_step4.png)

Verify that the application updates to display data and notifications related to the selected brand.
