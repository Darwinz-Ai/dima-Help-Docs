Location is a physical location that you want to track on Dima. The physical location is a Google Maps place which you can monitor all reviews for.

### [1\. Select the company you want to add the location to, click on the photo of the company](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

\[the scroll bar to navigate with through the different companies in the brands page is via the grey scrollbar on the rightmost of the page\]

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/05120a9b-2e9b-4450-a534-e92ab3cc2af4.png?0)

### [2\. use the scrollbar to scroll down to the manage tab \[has a settings icon directly above it\] and then click on that icon](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

the scrollbar is to the right of the sidebar and the side bar is on the left most part of the page

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/b9c2a58e-c206-47c1-b420-d3a1c5727809/1/1.5804036209981/86.808220850278?0)

### [3\. You can also select the company, where the location will be added to](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

When on "Company Locations" page ,a search bar with a list of companies related with the account signed up with

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9ecb9310-bfcd-4cd6-bf54-1908b2458c5d/1/50/50?0)

### [4\. Click on Company Locations tab \[located under the Own Pages (on the left) tab in the Manage page\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

to add/edit/delete locations related with the company chosen from Dima's platform

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/29a3ec21-f698-4806-a40c-311248847477/1/6.0416668653488/16.821918226268?0)

### [5\. Click on "Add Company Location" button on the top right of the screen](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

\[the button is underneath the company's logo, notifications icons\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c6da5b0e-b7a5-4444-8e15-e0f1bccfdc34/1/97.552084922791/11.726027188236?0)

### [6\. Click on the textbox under the label "Name"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

\[the label "Name" is located under the "Add Company Location" title of the page\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/d46442fe-2f18-4627-9fd1-a231d0279a6c/1/50.000000993411/42.657533410477?0)

### [7\. Type in the textbox,that you clicked at, the name of the location](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/18d904e8-0031-4a1e-bfe9-344a9e85bd5d/1/50.000000993411/53.509589366391?0)

### [8\. Click on textbox under the label "URL"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

the textbox is located at the bottom of the page, above the "Cancel" and "Add Company Location" buttons

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/2649b85c-bf26-4745-b9c5-10e5b034c098/1/50.000000993411/61.506849315068?0)

### [9\. Type in the textbox the URL of the location](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/bc84fcb3-1a2a-4e0c-97b8-d624971cfd2d/1/50.000000993411/61.506849315068?0)

### [10\. Click on Add Company Location](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

now it will be added to google reviews by Dima as Dima will fetch the google reviews of that location  
the button is located at the bottom right of the page

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a90703bf-b423-42f1-8225-76c9963e2fe7/1/90.052088101705/72.027399768568?0)
