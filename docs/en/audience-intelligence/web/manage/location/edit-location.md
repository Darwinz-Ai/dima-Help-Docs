dima allows the user to edit the company's locations. Start by going to the Manage module then click on the Locations tab then select the location you want to update.



### [1\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/b9c2a58e-c206-47c1-b420-d3a1c5727809/1/1.5804036209981/86.808220850278?0)

### [2\. Select the company, whose location will be edited](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

When on "Company Locations" page ,a search bar with a list of companies related with the account signed up with

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9ecb9310-bfcd-4cd6-bf54-1908b2458c5d/1/50/50?0)

### [3\. Click on Company Locations tab \[located under the Own Pages (on the left) tab in the Manage page\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

to add/edit/delete locations related with the company chosen from dima's platform

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/430c9ada-872e-46d7-a470-04a9a208c90d/1/0/0?0)

### [4\. Go to the company's location you want to update, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired company location to be updated](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit Company Location" or "Delete Company Location", click on "Edit Company Location"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/60f4d67d-fdfd-4c9d-ba13-4de73a4e8e44/1/72.656252980232/25.3150689112?0)

### [5\. Edit the desired feilds: Name, URL](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)


![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/b874f445-ffd1-4d1e-9d4b-216e93f80bf4/1/50.000000993411/42.657533410477?0)


### [6\. Click on Update Company Location](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

\[located at the bottom right of the page\]
