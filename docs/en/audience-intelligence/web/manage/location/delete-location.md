dima also allows the user to delete the company's location. This is the same flow of editing a company's location but instead of choosing to edit the company's location, you delete it.



### [1\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/b9c2a58e-c206-47c1-b420-d3a1c5727809/1/1.5804036209981/86.808220850278?0)



### [2\. Select the company you want to delete a location belonging to it](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)


![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/05120a9b-2e9b-4450-a534-e92ab3cc2af4.png?0)

### [3\. Click on Company Locations tab \[located under the Own Pages (on the left) tab in the Manage page\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

to add/edit/delete locations related with the company chosen from dima's platform

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/430c9ada-872e-46d7-a470-04a9a208c90d/1/0/0?0)

### [4\. Go to the company location you want to remove, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired company location to be removed](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit Company Location" or "Delete Company Location"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/60f4d67d-fdfd-4c9d-ba13-4de73a4e8e44/1/72.656252980232/25.3150689112?0)

### [6\. Click on Delete Company Location](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/5519659f-75d9-4633-9121-a26e2e08f517/1/72.135419646899/30.520548363254?0)

### [7\. Click on the Delete button](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/google-place)

\[located at the bottom right of the page\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/84c10133-8939-480f-a886-0e3e12a2aaec/1/65.052085618178/66.164383561644?0)
