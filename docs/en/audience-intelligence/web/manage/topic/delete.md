Dima allows users to delete topics. The process is by going to the Manage module and selecting the topics tab to select from the desired topic to be edited. Click on the 3 dotted button to delete the topic. Note: If this topic is associated with any posts, it will be removed from those posts as well and it cannot be undone.

### [1\. Go to the sidebar \[on the leftmost part of the page\] and click on the scroll bar](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

\[the scroll bar is located next to the side bar on it's right, colored with a shade of grey\]

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/5981eb57-8948-4c5a-b164-acb2919d8101.png?0)

### [2\. Pull the scroll bar down till you see the Manage tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/65d4c60a-1326-49d0-be65-4f22244b46ed/1/4.6744793653488/39.347603157775?0)

### [3\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c725bda1-5edb-41f7-99a2-079e07a65563/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)

### [4\. Click on Topics tab, to see the all topics created by the company](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

\[located to the right of the sidebar under the Dima logo\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9371d65b-2f50-4541-a443-7c40c266f061/1/6.0416668653488/33.534246993391?0)

### [5\. Go to the topic you want to edit it's details, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired topic](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/edit-topics)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit Topic" or "Delete Topic", click on "Delete Topic"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/690df986-a85e-4474-845a-60f6ff26123f/2.5/90.677085518837/71.287670396779?0)

### [6\. Click on the Delete button](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/edit-topics)

\[located at the bottom right of the pop-up\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/b0ac5e3e-2a2a-4389-b9c2-ae3c377eaed5/1/65.052085618178/66.164383561644?0)
