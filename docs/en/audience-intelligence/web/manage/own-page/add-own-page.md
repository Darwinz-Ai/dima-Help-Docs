Own pages include posts specifically by the official pages of the company on social media platforms, or pages owned and managed by the company being monitored. Own pages have a dedicated module in dima where you can view all the posts created by the different pages managed by each company being monitored and analyze their performance in terms of engagement, views and other social metrics as well as analyze best times for posting and the types of content being posted on each company's official pages. You can add new own pages or edit existing own page links from the Manage Module.


### [1\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY5JnRhYj1tb25pdG9y)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/47bb9962-4c2c-43df-94d9-c701bd8f5888/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)

### [2\. Click on the company you want to add in it the own page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

\[the companies are located in between the Manage section and Own Pages section with a search bar on top to narrow down on company searching\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/2d79af61-b14e-4e72-afe9-839047324dea/1/23.854166269302/33.671233294761?0)


### [3\. Fill in the required fields: Name, Platform, URL, "Track all posts on this page?"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a7ec4bb2-41b7-4085-943b-62fb3fa1f8da/1/33.588867386182/40.46575258856?0)


### [4\. If you don't want to track all posts on the group you want to add then click on the checkbox with the label "Track all posts in the groups?" and fill in the wanted posts to be tracked](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/5453541c-37f8-4298-b2f5-f347178913e1/1/9.9479168653488/59.86301369863?0)


### [5\. Click on "Add Own Page" button](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

\[located at the bottom right corner of the page\]
