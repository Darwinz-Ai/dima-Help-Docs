dima also allows you to remove users from the company. This is the same flow of editing a user but instead of choosing to edit the user, you delete the user.

### [1\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c725bda1-5edb-41f7-99a2-079e07a65563/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)

### [2\. Click on Users tab, located in the Manage section labelled with red](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/3d27ea9f-6295-4705-90ec-12c8fd5ed052/1/0/0?0)

### [3\. Go to the user you want to edit his details, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired user](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/users)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit User" or "Delete User"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/0dc735e4-8e3c-4139-90c7-9bc8bee9e4af/1/50.052086015542/45.260273136505?0)

### [4\. Click on Delete User button labelled in red](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/users)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/788cbce1-1dff-47c8-99ec-a371b806d5cb/1/50.052086015542/75.561648852205?0)

### [5\. Click on the Delete button to delete the user](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/users)

\[the button is on the bottom right of the pop-up\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9219be98-8877-4a2c-a9b9-8dea2a84549b/1/65.052085618178/60.575344137949?0)
