Dima allows the user to edit other users in the company. Start by going to the Manage module then click on the Users tab then select the user you want to update his information.

### [1\. Go to the sidebar \[on the leftmost part of the page\] and click on the scroll bar](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

\[the scroll bar is located next to the side bar on it's right, colored with a shade of grey\]

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/5981eb57-8948-4c5a-b164-acb2919d8101.png?0)

### [2\. Pull the scroll bar down till you see the Manage tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/65d4c60a-1326-49d0-be65-4f22244b46ed/1/4.6744793653488/39.347603157775?0)

### [3\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c725bda1-5edb-41f7-99a2-079e07a65563/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)

### [4\. Click on Users tab, located in the Manage section labelled with red](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/3d27ea9f-6295-4705-90ec-12c8fd5ed052/1/0/0?0)

### [5\. Go to the user you want to edit his details, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired user](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/users)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit User" or "Delete User", click on "Edit User"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/0dc735e4-8e3c-4139-90c7-9bc8bee9e4af/1/50.052086015542/45.260273136505?0)

### [6\. Click on the textbox labelled "Display Name" and enter the new display name](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/users/267/viewer)

the textbox is located under the textbox labelled Username

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/e09b0a5b-b75d-4c5a-956b-cafdee2a444c/1/52.343750993411/32.876712328767?0)

### [7\. Click on the textbox labelled "Contact email" and enter the new contact email](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/users/267/viewer)

the textbox is located under the textbox labelled "Display Name"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/3d546eb6-6cc7-43db-a8c0-20747d2af419/1/52.343750993411/32.876712328767?0)

### [8\. Click on the textbox labelled "Timezone"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/users/267/viewer)

the textbox is located under the textbox labelled "Contact email"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/42f36557-4759-481c-8a33-a1a1b9688400/1/52.343750993411/48.986302205961?0)

### [9\. Click on the desired UTC](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/users/267/viewer)

the arrow pointing downward(marked with a red circle) is used for scrolling, just hover the cursor over it and the rest of the UTC values will appear.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a84f444d-5468-4289-a480-689b4f24da49/1/0/0?0)

### [10\. Click on the Update User button](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/users/267/viewer)

\[located at the bottom right corner of the page\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/23ec5d51-b654-4db7-a934-94e507c9296a/1/99.166670441628/95.859592385488?0)
