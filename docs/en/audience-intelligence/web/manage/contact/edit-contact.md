<video controls=""><source src="https://dubble.so/media/video/d3d1b6a1-b773-4b65-8814-089377a17633" type="video/mp4"></video>

dima allows the user to edit contacts related to the company. Start by going to the Manage module then click on the Contacts tab then select the contact you want to update his information.

### [1\. Select the company you want to add the location to, click on the photo of the company](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

\[the scroll bar to navigate with through the different companies in the brands page is via the grey scrollbar on the rightmost of the page\]

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/05120a9b-2e9b-4450-a534-e92ab3cc2af4.png?0)

### [2\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c725bda1-5edb-41f7-99a2-079e07a65563/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)

### [3\. Click on Contacts tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

to add/edit/delete contacts related with the company chosen from dima's platform

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/6533ef09-a55e-406c-a404-e49abfb3096f/1/6.0416668653488/24.219178500241?0)

### [4\. Go to the contact you want to edit his details, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired user](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/contacts)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit Contact" or "Delete Contact", click on "Edit Contact"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/8ad95612-628d-4c46-9984-dfebf398c446/1/91.19791885217/23.780822753906?0)

### [5\. Edit the feilds you want to change: Name, Email, Phone Number](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/contacts)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/33318b78-90cf-42e0-ba3e-394e3e4d69cc/1/50.000000993411/35.972603575824?0)

### [6\. Click on the Update Contact button](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/contacts)

\[located at the bottom right of the page\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/35efe508-1b5c-4fe2-abbc-523d1e8b9bd0/1/90.052088101705/78.712333783711?0)

### [7\. A pop-up labelled "Update Successful" will appear at the bottom right corner of the page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/contacts)

indicating that the contact has been updated

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/84a8c8a7-3c33-4245-a38d-8f2e7a911b83/1/90.052088101705/78.712333783711?0)
