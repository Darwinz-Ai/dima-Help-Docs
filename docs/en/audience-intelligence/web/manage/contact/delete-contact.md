# Delete Contact

Dima also allows users to delete contacts from the platform, via going to the Manage module then going to the contacts tab which provides the contacts related to the selected company. There you can delete the contact via the 3 dots button.

### [1\. Go to the sidebar \[on the leftmost part of the page\] and click on the scroll bar](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

\[the scroll bar is located next to the side bar on it's right, colored with a shade of grey\]

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/5981eb57-8948-4c5a-b164-acb2919d8101.png?0)

### [2\. Pull the scroll bar down till you see the Manage tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/65d4c60a-1326-49d0-be65-4f22244b46ed/1/4.6744793653488/39.347603157775?0)

### [3\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c725bda1-5edb-41f7-99a2-079e07a65563/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)

### [4\. Click on "Contacts" tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

\[it is located to the right side of the sidebar under the Dima logo\], the Contacts tab is found under the Company Locations tab all of them are in

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/40e584cc-6a34-4b91-a810-fb1b74a2baa8/1/6.0416668653488/16.821918226268?0)

### [5\. Click on the 3 dots inside the contact box](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/contacts)

when mouse hovers over the 3 dots a small pop-up called "Contact actions" appear, here you can edit/delete the contact after clicking on the 3 dots but in our case we will delete the contact

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/91f165e7-3a39-4dbe-a6d9-0d8d2817acd7/1/76.648765802383/20.054795774695?0)

### [6\. Click on "Delete Contact" written in red](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/contacts)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/52780e43-a324-4a51-a5e2-8ff31ffbe415/1/90.677085518837/28.986302205961?0)

### [7\. Click on the "Delete" button to remove the contact](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/contacts)

the button is located at the bottom right corner of the "Delete Contact" page

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/22c20c72-b8e9-46d0-b1eb-6e85e95cd551/1/65.052085618178/63.073630136986?0)
