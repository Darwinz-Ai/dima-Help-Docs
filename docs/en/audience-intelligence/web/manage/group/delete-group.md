dima also allows user to delete added Facebook groups on dima's platform. This is the same flow of editing a Facebook group but instead of choosing to edit the group, you delete it.

### [1\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c725bda1-5edb-41f7-99a2-079e07a65563/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)

### [2\. Click on the Groups tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/711eb951-e591-44b0-9d1d-f9d227a065af/1.5/6.0416668653488/28.876712746816?0)

### [3\. Go to the Group you want to delete, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired Group](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit Group" or "Delete Group"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/8ea264bd-f671-4d4c-9c87-9938f95af5ca/1/99.947920441628/39.013701138431?0)

### [4\. Click on Delete Group](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9630c632-7cf6-4211-949c-151f2873d1ac/1/72.135419646899/60.876711492669?0)

### [5\. Click on the Delete button](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/6ed52a62-3d7d-440d-ac77-d5315ac39b13/1/65.052085618178/66.164383561644?0)
