Groups are specific to Facebook platform. On Facebook, there is a group feature which is kind of like an open social community where Facebook users discuss topics. Each group is usually surrounded by a theme or topic, e.g. Foodies in  London, so that users on the group have something in common to discuss on the group (e.g. graduated from a specific university, or live in the same city or are interested in news about the same activity etc.) On dima, you can add public group links to monitor what 's being said by Facebook users within these groups or communities and use it for your analysis and reporting.

### [1\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c725bda1-5edb-41f7-99a2-079e07a65563/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)

### [2\. Click on the Groups tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/711eb951-e591-44b0-9d1d-f9d227a065af/1.5/6.0416668653488/28.876712746816?0)

### [3\. Click on the Add Group button \[located at the top right of the page\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/dedc0804-6ba0-46e2-bdec-1da71e26ce05/1/97.552084922791/11.726027188236?0)

### [4\. Fill in the required fields: Name, URL, "Track all posts in this group?"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/efef75b9-5736-4d6e-b7f9-d3ab0381829f/1/50.000000993411/42.109588204998?0)


### [5\. From here you can go to step 6 or 8](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

Depending on wether you want to track all posts on the group to be added or not

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/43a8d8c0-7258-4f7d-90e9-27050bace137/1/50.000000993411/57.671232876712?0)

### [6\. If you don't want to track all posts on the group you want to add then click on the checkbox with the label "Track all posts in the groups?"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/4883e3f0-30d0-41c5-9f4d-4bbdb072b3cd/1/0/0?0)

### [7\. Click on the textbox labelled Comment and write what type of posts you want to track from the group](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/5570a7c3-35d2-4d2e-ae84-ceee48f94665/1/50.000000993411/71.041096203948?0)

### [8\. Click on Add Group button \[on the bottom right of Add Group page\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)
