### [1\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c725bda1-5edb-41f7-99a2-079e07a65563/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)

### [2\. Click on the Groups tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/711eb951-e591-44b0-9d1d-f9d227a065af/1.5/6.0416668653488/28.876712746816?0)

### [3\. Go to the Group you want to edit it's details, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired Group](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit Group" or "Delete Group", click on "Edit Group"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/8ea264bd-f671-4d4c-9c87-9938f95af5ca/1/99.947920441628/39.013701138431?0)

### [4\. Edit the desired fields to be chnaged: Name, URL, "Track all posts in the group?"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/5be240cd-62e9-482c-951a-0b91c689b045/1/0/0?0)


### [5\. If you don't want to track all posts on the group you want to add then click on the checkbox with the label "Track all posts in the groups?"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/7967ba2d-64f0-4b37-9291-3b9b248bb1ab/1/0/0?0)

### [6\. Click on the textbox labelled Comment and write what type of posts you want to track from the updated Facebook group](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a720238d-9e2b-4e23-9749-7e2fb2e47e3a/1/0/0?0)

### [7\. Click on Update Group at the bottom right of the page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/groups)
