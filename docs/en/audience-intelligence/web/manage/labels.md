# Managing Labels

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/manage/labels/manage-labels-en.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

A **Label** is a custom tag used to organize your social intelligence data. Adding labels makes it significantly easier to filter mentions, categorize posts, and track specific marketing campaigns across your monitor pages.

Labels can be set up for either automated content classification or manual tagging. You can manage your entire label roster directly from the Manage page.

---

## Accessing the Labels Menu

![Screenshot of the Manage module showing the company selection list and the Labels tab](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/manage/labels/manage-labels-en-1.png)

All label management for a specific brand happens in one central location:

1. Navigate to the main sidebar and click the **Manage** icon.

2. Select the specific **Company** you want to manage from your connected brands list.

3. Click the **Labels** tab located in the secondary menu.

---

## Adding a Label

1. From the Labels tab, click the **Add Label** button in the top right corner of the page.

2. A form will appear. Fill in the fields:

   - **Name:** The title of the label (e.g., "Black Friday 2026", "Product Feedback").
   - **Description (Optional):** Contextual keywords or rules for AI classification.

   > **Note:** Providing a **Description** enables automatic post labeling across incoming mentions. If you leave this field empty, the label will be marked with a **"Manual Only"** tag in the Manage screen—meaning team members must manually navigate to individual posts on monitor pages to apply it.

3. Click the **Add Label** button at the bottom right corner of the form to save the new tag to your list.

---

## Editing a Label

![Screenshot showing the three-dot menu on a Label card with the Edit and Delete options](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/manage/labels/manage-labels-en-2.png)

If you need to update a label's title or adjust its automated classification description:

1. Locate the specific label card you want to update.

2. Click the **three-dot menu ( ⋮ )** in the top right corner of that specific card.

3. Select **Edit Label** from the dropdown menu.

4. Update the Name or Description fields as needed. Adding a description to a "Manual Only" label will enable automated post processing for future mentions.

5. Click **Update Label** in the bottom right corner to save your changes. A success message will appear at the bottom right of the screen confirming the update.

---

## Deleting a Label

If a campaign concludes or a tag is no longer needed, you can safely remove it:

1. Locate the specific label card you want to remove.

2. Click the **three-dot menu ( ⋮ )** in the top right corner of the card.

3. Select **Delete Label** (written in red) from the dropdown menu.

4. A confirmation pop-up will appear to ensure you want to proceed. Click the **Delete** button at the bottom right of the pop-up to permanently remove the label.
