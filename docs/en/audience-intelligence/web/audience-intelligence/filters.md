Dima allows users to filter posts on the Own Page, Groups and Audience Intelligence modules through several factors including: Sentiment, Emotion, Topic, Label, Platform, Language, Region and Gender.

### [1\. Click on Filter button located at the top right corner of the page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[labelled with the red arrow\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/6c9e1660-5de9-4c42-94bf-56970a93d236/1/0/0?0)

### [2\. In Filters tab you have 4 tabs to further filter with they are: Sentiment & Emotion, Topics & Labels, Platforms & Post Type, Demographic](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

in the Sentiment & Emotion tab you simply click on the options you want to filter with in Sentiment and Emotion categories  
\[you can select one or more options\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/32265a41-071b-4f50-9a7f-2d202950c2bb/1/24.881998697917/26.301369863014?0)

### [3\. Click on Topic & Labels tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click on the textbox/dropdown menu labelled "Topic"  
you can type in the desired topic or select it by clicking on it from the dropdown menu

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/426d95de-2ea5-4069-b2bc-7baee9eb6e17/1/24.947917461395/48.767123287671?0)

### [4\. Click on the textbox/dropdown menu labelled "Label"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

you can type in the desired topic or select it by clicking on it from the dropdown menu

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/8756e5ac-55df-48ea-9b28-89d4b5fa0bf1/1/24.947917461395/52.054794520548?0)

### [5\. Click on Platform & Post Type tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

you can select the desired platform by click on it.  
The user can also be able to write/select the name of the own pages (if user opened the filter from the Own Page tab) or to writing the link of the Facebook group (if user opened the filter from the Groups tab) \[both options are located under the Platform category\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/b6578054-194f-4073-9d85-fd6ef170419f/1/24.881998697917/26.301369863014?0)

### [6\. Click on Demographic tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/3c3013d5-684f-4a32-99b3-ff1539c1fcc4/1/24.947917461395/42.191780821918?0)

### [7\. Click on the textbox/dropdown labelled Language](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

you can type in the textbox for narrowing down or select the desired language, you can select one or many

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/61d2a140-06b1-468a-ae82-4f818b4fae13/1/81.406251589457/40?0)

### [8\. Click on the textbox/dropdown labelled Region](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

you can type in the textbox for narrowing down or select the desired language, you can select one or many

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/7bfcb1d5-5896-4a08-94f0-a7dd242bdc91/1/81.406251589457/40?0)

### [9\. Click on desired Gender](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/10206c1b-0e47-4447-b87d-bb1515ae5949/1/38.960774739583/61.041095681386?0)

### [10\. Click on Apply button to apply the filter](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[located at the bottom right corner of the page\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/ad207ac3-d4d3-4f9c-9983-40a0c6eeee77/1/81.718751788139/85.895552961794?0)

### [11\. Click on the icon in between the Filter and Collection buttons \[at the top right corner of the page\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[labelled with a red arrow\]

it shows the filters in a expanded fashion all of the selection criteria in one page
