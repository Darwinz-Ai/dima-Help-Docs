# Catching Up with Trend Stories

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/story/stories-en.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

**Trend Analysis** shows you what a country is talking about on social media over the last 24 hours. dima follows the country's trending list every hour and checks the top trends on it (roughly the top 30), keeping only the real conversations: bot campaigns, filler phrases (greetings, dates) and conversations that really belong to another country are filtered out before they reach you. Trends are checked again during the day, so one can still be filtered out later if it turns out not to be genuine.

For every trend, dima tells you what it is actually about (not just the name it trends under), how high it reached, how long it has stayed on the list, how many people are posting about it, and whether it is climbing or fading right now.

> **Note:** Throughout Trend Analysis, **"today"** means **the last 24 hours**, not since midnight. For example, **New today** covers the trends that appeared in the last 24 hours, whatever the time of day.

> **Beta:** Trend Analysis is currently in beta, so some parts may still change.

## 1. Opening Trend Analysis

![Screenshot of the Trend Analysis page open on the Story tab, with the Story and Explore tabs, the story chips, the story player and the posts strip labelled](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/story/story-tab.png)

1. Open the [Trend Analysis](/en/trend-analysis) page from the main sidebar.
2. The page opens on the **Story** tab. Use the tabs at the top to switch between:
   - **Story:** a short recap of today's most interesting trends, one trend at a time. Start here for a quick catch-up. This guide covers it.
   - **Explore:** the full list of today's trends, where you can search, filter, sort and open any trend's details. Learn how to use it in **[Exploring Trends](/en/guides/trend-analysis/explore)**.

---

## 2. Choosing a Story

The **Story** tab picks a handful of trends that each tell you something about today, and plays them one after the other, like stories on social media.

The row of chips at the top lists today's stories. Each one is a different kind of moment:

| Story               | Shows                                                                            |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Top trend**       | The trend highest on the trending list right now.                                |
| **Biggest climber** | The trend that moved up the list the most in the last 3 hours.                   |
| **New today**       | The highest trend that appeared on the list in the last 24 hours.                |
| **Sleeper**         | A small trend that isn't high on the list yet, but whose posts are growing fast. |
| **Cooling**         | The trend that dropped down the list the most in the last 3 hours.               |
| **Left the list**   | A trend that reached the top earlier and has since left the list.                |

Click a chip to play that story, or let them play in order.

## 3. What a Story Shows

![Screenshot of the story player with the Headline card on the left, the stats card on the right and the Up next chip under it](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/story/story-player.png)

A playing story is split into two cards and a chip:

- **Headline card (left):** what the trend is really about, the name it trends under, and why it was picked (e.g., _"Up 10 places in 3 hours"_). It stays in place for the whole story.
- **Stats card (right):** plays the trend's figures one after the other, with a progress bar at the top. It only shows the ones dima has data for:
  - **The climb:** how the trend's position on the trending list changed through the day, when it entered the top 10, and the best spot it reached.
  - **The pulse:** how many posts were written about it in the last 24 hours, and its busiest hour.
  - **Where it's trending:** the countries where it reached the trending list, when it trends in more than one.
- **Up next chip (under the stats card):** the next story in line. Click it to skip ahead.

Hover over the stats card to pause it. Click anywhere on it to move forward, or on its left third to go back.

Under the player, a strip of posts **From the conversation** shows what people are actually saying. Hover over it to pause it, and click a post to open the original.

> **Tip:** Want to know exactly what a trend's position, movement or posts mean? See **[Reading a trend card](/en/guides/trend-analysis/explore#2-reading-a-trend-card)**.

---

## 4. Finishing Up

A story counts as watched once you move on from it. When you've seen them all, dima lets you know you're caught up. New stories appear as the trending list moves through the day.

To dig deeper into a trend, click **Open in Explore**: dima switches to the Explore tab with that trend's details open. Learn what the details show in **[Viewing a Trend's Details](/en/guides/trend-analysis/explore#4-viewing-a-trends-details)**.
