# Exploring Trends

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/explore-en.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

The **Explore** tab of [Trend Analysis](/en/trend-analysis) lists every trend from the last 24 hours, including the ones that have already ended. It's where you read a trend's numbers, see what is moving right now, and open any trend to dig deeper.

To get there, open Trend Analysis from the main sidebar and click the **Explore** tab. New to Trend Analysis? Start with **[Catching Up with Trend Stories](/en/guides/trend-analysis/story)**.

> **Note:** Throughout Trend Analysis, **"today"** means **the last 24 hours**, not since midnight. For example, **Posts today** counts the posts from the last 24 hours, whatever the time of day.

---

## 1. The Explore Tab

![Screenshot of the Explore tab with the filter bar, highlights, action bar, category summary, trend cards and Moving now list labelled](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/explore-tab.png)

From top to bottom, the Explore tab has:

1. **The filter bar:** search, country and **Sync data**.
2. **Highlights:** a quick count of today's trends.
3. **The action bar:** switch between the grid and the race view, and change the sort order.
4. **Category summary:** how today's trends split across categories.
5. **Trend cards:** one card per trend.
6. **Moving now:** the biggest moves of the last 3 hours.

To narrow down or reorder the cards, see **[Filtering and Sorting Trends](/en/guides/trend-analysis/filters)**.

### Highlights

![Screenshot of the highlights row with the Trends, On the list now, Gaining, Holding, Cooling and New today counters](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/highlights.png)

The row of counters at the top sums up today's trends at a glance:

- **Trends:** every trend in the list today.
- **On the list now:** how many of them are on the latest hourly trending list.
- **Gaining**, **Holding** and **Cooling:** how many are moving up, staying put or moving down (explained under [Movement](#movement) below).
- **New today:** how many first appeared on the list in the last 24 hours.

### Category summary

![Screenshot of the category summary showing the number of trends per category](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/category-summary.png)

The **Category summary** shows how many trends fall into each category, such as _Sports_, _Business_, _Entertainment_ or _General_.

> **Note:** Many trends are labelled **General**, because the category comes from the trending list itself, which often doesn't give a more specific one.

## 2. Reading a Trend Card

![Screenshot of a single trend card with its title, trending names, movement badge, ranks, posts, reach and posts-per-hour chart labelled](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/trend-card-anatomy.png)

Each card is one trend. Here is what each part means.

### Title and "Trending as"

The large **title** describes what the trend is really about (e.g., _"FlyDubai emergency landing in Tabuk"_). The **Trending as** line shows the actual name or hashtag people are using (e.g., _تبوك_).

One news event often trends under several names at once. dima groups them into a single card and lists all of its names under **Trending as**, instead of showing several cards for the same event.

### Country

When you view **All countries**, each card also shows the country the trend belongs to. When you view a single country, the country is left out, since every card belongs to it.

### Ranks

![Diagram of a trend's position over the last 24 hours, showing where its Now, Today and Peak ranks come from](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/explore-ranks-explained.png)

The trending list is a ranking that updates every hour, so a trend's position keeps changing. The card describes it with three numbers, each answering a different question:

| Label         | Question it answers          | What it means                                                                                                     |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Now #13**   | Where is it right now?       | Its position on the **latest** hourly list. If it isn't on the latest list, the card says so.                     |
| **#3 today**  | How big was it over the day? | Its position over the **last 24 hours** of hourly lists. A trend that stays near the top for longer ranks higher. |
| **Peak rank** | How high did it get?         | The **best position** it ever reached.                                                                            |

### Movement

![Diagram comparing a trend's position 3 hours ago with now for each movement badge](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/explore-movement-explained.png)

The movement badge tells you what is happening to the trend **now, compared with about 3 hours ago**. A change of 3 places or more counts as a move.

| Badge            | Meaning                                                            | Example                         |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| **Just started** | It appeared on the trending list for the first time recently.      | _new since 13:00, now #9_       |
| **Gaining**      | It moved up the list, or came back to the list after dropping off. | _up 10 places in 3h, now #4_    |
| **Holding**      | It stayed around the same position.                                | _steady around #5_              |
| **Cooling**      | It moved down the list.                                            | _down 10 places in 3h, now #13_ |
| **Ended**        | It is no longer on the trending list.                              | _left the trending list 2h ago_ |

Trends that have been tracked for only a few hours have no badge yet, because there is nothing to compare with.

> **Note:** Seeing many **Ended** trends is normal. The Explore tab shows the trends from the last 24 hours, including ones that have already ended. A card's position reflects the whole day; its badge reflects what is happening right now.

### Posts today and the Posts/hour chart

![Diagram of the four size tiers, from Small to Huge, with the number of posts each one covers](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/explore-size-tiers.png)

**Posts today** is the number of posts about the trend in the last 24 hours, reposts included. For a trend with several names, the posts of all its names are added together. dima also gives each trend a size:

| Size       | Posts in the last 24 hours |
| ---------- | -------------------------- |
| **Small**  | under 1,000                |
| **Medium** | 1,000 – 9,999              |
| **Large**  | 10,000 – 49,999            |
| **Huge**   | 50,000 or more             |

The small **Posts/hour** bar chart on the card shows how the conversation grew hour by hour, with the busiest hour highlighted.

Some trends show **"Not counted yet"**. This happens when a trend is new and hasn't been counted, or when its name is too common a word to count reliably.

> **Note:** Time on the list doesn't mean size. Two trends can both stay on the list for 22 hours, one with 44,000 posts and the other with only a few dozen. Use **Posts today** to judge how big a trend is.

### Reach

When a trend's posts haven't been counted yet, the card shows **Reach** in place of **Posts today**. Reach is also shown as a size (Small to Huge), based on the views of the sample posts dima collected for the trend. Treat it as a minimum, not as an audience size: the real number of people who saw the trend is higher.

Click any card to open its [details](#4-viewing-a-trends-details).

## 3. Moving Now

![Screenshot of the Moving now list with the Gaining now and Cooling off sections](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/moving-now.png)

The **Moving now** list shows the biggest moves of the last 3 hours, using the same movement as the badges:

- **Gaining now:** trends that just appeared (**new**), came back to the list (**back**), or climbed (e.g., **▲ 10** for 10 places up).
- **Cooling off:** trends that dropped (e.g., **▼ 10**) or recently left the list (e.g., **ended 2h ago**).

Each row starts with the trend's position on the list right now (or **—** if it isn't on it).

## 4. Viewing a Trend's Details

![Screenshot of the trend details side panel showing the header, figures, rank chart, map and sample posts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/trend-details.png)

Clicking a card (or **Open in Explore** in a story) opens the **Trend details** panel. On smaller screens it opens as a sheet from the bottom. Scroll inside it to explore:

- **Header:** the title in English and Arabic, the names it trends under, and how long it has been on the list (or how long it trended for, if it has ended).
- **Figures:** the card's numbers (**Now**, **Today**, **Peak rank**, **Posts today**, **Reach**), plus **On the list for** and **Engagement per post**. Hover over a figure to see what it means and the exact numbers behind it.
- **Position per hourly list:** a chart of the trend's position on each hourly list through the day, with #1 at the top. For a trend with several names, it shows the best position any of its names held.
- **Where it's trending:** a map of the countries where the trend reached the trending list.
- **Sample posts:** the posts with the most engagement, from all of the trend's names.

## 5. Watching the Trend Race

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/explore/trend-race.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

The race view replays the last 24 hours as an animated bar chart of the **top 10 trends by posts so far today**, so you can see which conversations took over at which point in the day.

1. In the action bar, switch the view from the grid to **Race view**.
2. Click the play button (**Play the last 24 hours**) to watch the race hour by hour, or drag along the timeline to jump to a specific hour.
3. Next to each bar, you can see whether the trend gained or lost places since the hour before, or whether it is **New**.

Each colour stands for a category, listed in the legend under the race.

> **Note:** Only trends whose posts have been counted take part in the race.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why don't I see a trend I saw on social media?**
dima only shows trends that passed its checks. Trends that look like bot campaigns, filler phrases (greetings, dates), conversations from another country, or trends with too little data are filtered out. New trends are also checked hourly, so a very recent one may not appear yet.

**Why is the Explore tab almost empty, or full of ended trends?**
Early in the day (or after a quiet night), many trends on the new list haven't been checked yet. They appear as soon as they are checked, every hour.

**What happens if a trend comes back after it ended?**
It continues on the same card. If it is still in the list when it returns, its badge changes from **Ended** to **Gaining** (_back on the list_).

**Why does a trend say "Not counted yet"?**
Its posts haven't been counted yet, or its name is too common a word to count reliably. Use its ranks and reach in the meantime.

**Why is a trend's reach a size and not a number?**
Reach comes from a sample of posts, so the exact number would understate the real audience. The exact views are available when you hover over the reach.
