# Filtering and Sorting Trends

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/filters/filters-en.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

The **Explore** tab of [Trend Analysis](/en/trend-analysis) can hold a lot of trends. Use search, the filters and the sort order to find the ones you care about.

The filters here are specific to Trend Analysis. To learn what each part of a trend means (movement, ranks, posts), see **[Exploring Trends](/en/guides/trend-analysis/explore)**.

---

## 1. The Filter Bar

![Screenshot of the filter bar with the search field, the country picker, the Sync data button and the Filters button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/filters/filter-bar.png)

- **Search:** type to find trends by their title or by any of the names they trend under, in English or Arabic.
- **Country:** the country whose trending list you are viewing: **Egypt**, **Saudi Arabia**, **UAE** or **Oman**. Pick **All countries** to see the trends of every country together, with each country's trends mixed in by their order. Each card then shows its country.
- **Sync data:** loads the latest trending list. dima checks new trends every hour, starting with the highest on the list.

---

## 2. Filters

![Screenshot of the Filters dialog with the Movement, Category and Trending as tabs](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/filters/filters-dialog.png)

1. Click the **Filters** button.
2. Pick what to keep from each tab. You can select several options in each:
   - **Movement:** _Just started_, _Gaining_, _Holding_, _Cooling_ or _Ended_ (see [Movement](/en/guides/trend-analysis/explore#movement)).
   - **Category:** only the categories that have trends today are listed.
   - **Trending as:** the kind of name the trend uses: a **Hashtag** (starts with #), a **Keyword** (a single word) or a **Phrase** (several words).
3. Click **Apply**. To clear your selections, click **Reset**.

If no trend matches your filters, the Explore tab says so: click **Clear filters** to start again.

---

## 3. Sorting

![Screenshot of the Sort by menu with its five options](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/trend-analysis/filters/sort-menu.png)

Use the **Sort by** menu in the action bar to change the order of the cards:

| Option                  | Puts first                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Longest on the list** | Trends that stayed on the trending list the longest. _(Default)_                                                                                      |
| **Most important**      | What matters most in the country right now, combining posts, reach, time on the list and momentum. Trends that have ended slowly sink down the order. |
| **Most posts today**    | Trends with the most posts in the last 24 hours.                                                                                                      |
| **Most reach**          | Trends whose sample posts got the most views.                                                                                                         |
| **Newest**              | Trends that appeared most recently.                                                                                                                   |

Trends without a value for the chosen order (e.g., posts not counted yet) are placed at the end.
