# AI Visibility Overview

More and more of your customers ask AI engines like **ChatGPT**, **Gemini**, **Perplexity** and **Google's AI Overviews** for advice before they buy. **AI Visibility** shows you whether those answers mention your brand, how they describe you, and which competitors appear next to you.

The **Overview** sums up a month at a glance: your main scores, the topics where you're falling behind, and recommendations on what to do next.

> **Note:** If the page says _No AI visibility audits yet_, it hasn't been set up for your company. Click **Send Mail** to contact the dima team.

---

## 1. Opening AI Visibility

![Screenshot of the AI Visibility Overview with the market, AI engine and month selectors, the scores, the topics and the recommendations](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/overview/overview-page.png)

1. Open **AI Visibility** from the main sidebar.
2. Click **Overview**. The other views are **AI Perception** and **Trends**.

AI Visibility looks at the answers AI engines give to the questions, or **prompts**, people in your market ask about your industry.

## 2. Choosing a Market, AI Engine and Month

<video autoplay loop muted playsinline controls aria-label="Switching the Market, AI engine and Month selectors at the top of the page"><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/overview/selectors.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

The selectors at the top of the page decide which answers you're looking at:

- **Market:** the country you're looking at. It only appears when your company is followed in more than one market. A market marked _Paused_ or _Closed_ no longer gets new results.
- **AI engine:** **ChatGPT**, **Gemini**, **Perplexity** or **AI Overviews**. Each engine answers differently, so its numbers are shown on their own.
- **Month:** the results are grouped by month. A month marked _In progress_ is still collecting answers, so its numbers will change.

To narrow the answers further, for example to Arabic prompts only, click **Filters**. [Learn how to filter AI Visibility here.](/en/guides/ai-visibility/filters)

## 3. Reading This Month's Warnings

![Screenshot of the "Read this month with care" banner listing why the month's numbers may not compare cleanly](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/overview/warnings.png)

When something about the month makes it harder to compare with the month before, a **Read this month with care** banner explains why:

- The month is still running, so its numbers will change.
- The AI engine switched to a new model, or more than one model version answered.
- The prompts changed since last month.
- The brands, prompt topics or personas being followed changed since last month.

The numbers are still accurate. The banner just reminds you that a change from last month may come from these, and not only from how AI sees your brand.

## 4. Reading Your Scores

![Screenshot of the three scores: Mention rate, Share of voice and Positive mentions](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/overview/scores.png)

The three scores at the top sum up the month. Hover over the **?** next to a score to see what it means.

- **Mention rate:** the share of AI answers that mention you. Below it, you can see how it moved since last month.
- **Share of voice:** your share of all the brand mentions, compared with your competitors. Below it, you can see where you rank among the brands, e.g., _Ranked #2 of 5 brands_.
- **Positive mentions:** the share of your mentions that are positive.

![Diagram showing the difference between mention rate, the share of answers that mention you, and share of voice, your share of all brand mentions](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/overview/overview-mention-rate-vs-share-of-voice.png)

> **Tip:** The two scores answer different questions. **Mention rate** tells you how often AI brings you up at all. **Share of voice** tells you how much of the conversation you get compared with your competitors. You can have a high mention rate but a low share of voice when your competitors are mentioned even more.

Below the scores, **What moved this month** sums up the most important changes since last month. It covers all the AI engines together, so its numbers can differ from the scores above, which are for the selected engine only. It doesn't appear in the first month, since there's nothing to compare yet.

## 5. Finding Where You're Falling Behind

![Screenshot of the Mention rate by prompt topic card, with a topic marked Falling behind](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/overview/topics.png)

The **Mention rate by prompt topic** card breaks your mention rate down by the topic of the prompts, such as prices or customer service. For each topic, it shows:

- **You:** the share of the topic's answers that mention you.
- Your **top competitor** on that topic, and the share of answers that mention them.
- How many answers the topic is based on.

A topic is marked **Falling behind** when your top competitor's mention rate on it is at least 20 points higher than yours, e.g., 65% against your 40%. These topics are listed first, so you can see where to focus. Click **Show all** to see every topic.

## 6. Following the Recommendations

![Screenshot of the Recommendations card with a recommendation, its "What to do" step and the engines it's based on](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/overview/recommendations.png)

The **Recommendations** card suggests what to do to show up more often, and more positively, in AI answers. Each recommendation shows:

- What dima found, and why it matters.
- **What to do:** the step to take. Click **Show more** to read the full text.

At the bottom of the card, **Based on** lists the AI engines the recommendations come from. Engines still collecting answers for the month are listed under **Not included yet**.

> **Note:** Recommendations cover all the AI engines together for the selected month, so they don't change when you pick another engine or set filters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does the page say "No results for this market yet"?**
Your company is set up for this market, but no AI answers have been collected yet. The results show up after the first run.

**Why does it say "No data for this selection"?**
There are no answers for this AI engine and month yet. Try another engine or month.

**Why is there no change shown under my mention rate?**
It's the first month for this engine, so there's nothing to compare with yet.

**Why are my numbers different on each AI engine?**
Each engine gives its own answers, from its own sources. That's why each engine's numbers are shown on their own.
