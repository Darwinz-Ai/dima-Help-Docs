# AI Visibility Trends

One month tells you where you stand. **Trends** shows where you're heading: how your mention rate, share of voice, sentiment and sources have changed month by month, next to your competitors.

---

## 1. Opening Trends

![Screenshot of the Trends page with its four charts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/trends/trends-page.png)

1. Open **AI Visibility** from the main sidebar.
2. Click **Trends**.

Trends shows every month for the selected **Market** and **AI engine**, so the **Month** selector is greyed out here. Your filters still apply. [Learn how to filter AI Visibility here.](/en/guides/ai-visibility/filters)

> **Note:** Trends needs at least two months of results. Until then, it doesn't appear in the sidebar. If one AI engine only has a single month so far, try another engine.

---

## 2. Reading the Charts

![Screenshot of the Mention rate over time chart, with your brand's line drawn thicker than your competitors'](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/trends/mention-rate-chart.png)

Trends has four charts:

- **Mention rate over time:** the share of answers that mention each brand, month by month.
- **Share of voice over time:** each brand's share of all the brand mentions, month by month.
- **Your mention rate by prompt topic:** how often you're mentioned in the answers to each prompt topic.
- **Sentiment and sources over time:** your share of **Positive mentions**, and your **Own-page share** of the sources AI cites.

In the first two charts, your brand's line is drawn thicker than your competitors'.

- Hover over a chart to see each month's exact values.
- Hover over a name in the legend to highlight its line and fade the others.

[Learn what each of these scores means here.](/en/guides/ai-visibility/overview)

## 3. Spotting Months That Need a Closer Look

![Screenshot of a trend chart with a dashed line marking a month with a warning, and its tooltip explaining why](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/trends/warning-months.png)

A **dashed line** marks a month with a warning, for example when the AI engine switched to a new model or the prompts changed. Hover over that month to see why.

A sudden jump or drop in one of these months may come from that change, and not only from how AI sees your brand.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why can't I find Trends in the sidebar?**
The selected market only has one month of results so far. Trends appears once there are at least two.

**Why does Trends say "Not enough months for a trend"?**
The selected AI engine only has one month so far. Try another engine in the meantime.

**Why does Sentiment and sources over time say its history isn't available?**
The monthly sentiment and source history isn't available for this selection yet. You can still see each month's sentiment and sources in **[AI Perception](/en/guides/ai-visibility/ai-perception)**.
