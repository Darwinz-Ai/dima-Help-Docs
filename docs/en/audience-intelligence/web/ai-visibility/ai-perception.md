# AI Perception

Being mentioned by AI is only half the story. **AI Perception** shows _how_ AI engines talk about your brand: whether they're positive, whether they recommend you or just list you, what they associate you with compared with your competitors, and which websites they rely on. You can also read exactly what AI wrote, quote by quote.

---

## 1. Opening AI Perception

![Screenshot of the AI Perception page with the Sentiment, How you are mentioned and Quotes cards](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/ai-perception/ai-perception-page.png)

1. Open **AI Visibility** from the main sidebar.
2. Click **AI Perception**.

Everything on the page follows the **Market**, **AI engine** and **Month** at the top, and your filters. [Learn how to choose them in the AI Visibility Overview.](/en/guides/ai-visibility/overview)

## 2. Sentiment and How You Are Mentioned

![Screenshot of the Sentiment and How you are mentioned cards next to the Quotes card, where a quote shows the prompt and the AI engine answer with the brand name highlighted](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/ai-perception/sentiment-roles-quotes.png)

**Sentiment** shows how AI talks about you when it mentions you: **Positive**, **Neutral**, **Negative** or **Mixed**.

> **Tip:** Click a sentiment to read the quotes behind it. For example, click **Negative** to see every negative thing AI said about you this month.

**How you are mentioned** shows the part your brand plays in the answers, from the strongest to the weakest:

- **Recommended:** AI suggests you as a good choice.
- **Listed:** AI names you among other options, without recommending you.
- **Passing mention:** AI names you briefly, without discussing you.
- **Cautioned against:** AI advises against choosing you.

Each part shows how many mentions it covers. Hover over a part of the bar or the list to highlight it.

---

## 3. Reading the Quotes

The **Quotes** card shows what AI actually wrote. Each quote looks like a chat: the prompt that was asked, then the part of the AI engine's answer that mentions the brand, with the brand's name highlighted.

- **Brand:** pick whose quotes to read. It starts on your brand, but you can read what AI says about any of your competitors too.
- **Sentiment:** pick one or more sentiments to only read those quotes.
- Use the arrows at the top of the card to move between pages of quotes.

## 4. What AI Associates With Each Brand

![Screenshot of the attributes heatmap, with one row per attribute and one column per brand](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/ai-perception/attributes.png)

The **What AI associates with each brand** table shows the attributes AI brings up in its answers, such as price, quality or service, and how positive it is about each brand on each one. Your brand is the first column.

- **The color** of a cell shows how positive AI is about that brand on that attribute: **Mostly positive** (60% or more), **Mixed** (40% to 59%) or **Mostly negative** (under 40%).
- **The number** is the share of what AI says that's positive, next to the number of mentions behind it. Hover over a cell to see the exact positive and negative counts.
- **Faded cells** are based on very few mentions, so read them with care.
- **A dash** means AI didn't mention that brand for that attribute.
- **A red dot** next to an attribute means a competitor is described clearly more positively than you on it.

![Diagram explaining how to read a cell of the attributes heatmap: its color, its numbers, faded cells and the competitor warning](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/ai-perception/ai-perception-attributes-heatmap.png)

The attributes AI talks about most for your brand are listed first. Brands that weren't mentioned for any attribute are listed under the table.

## 5. Sources AI Cites

![Screenshot of the Sources AI cites card with the own-page share, the source types and the top domains](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/ai-perception/sources.png)

AI engines often back up their answers with links. The **Sources AI cites** card shows where that information comes from:

- **Own-page share:** how many of the cited sources are your own pages. The higher it is, the more AI relies on what you say about yourself.
- **By type:** the kinds of websites cited, such as **Your pages**, **Competitor pages**, **Social media**, **Forums**, **News**, **Review sites**, **Directories**, **Benchmarks** and **Blogs**.
- **Top domains:** the websites cited most often.

> **Tip:** If review sites or forums make up a big part of the sources, what people say about you there has a direct effect on what AI says about you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does a card say "No data this month"?**
There weren't enough answers mentioning your brand for that card, with your current engine, month and filters. Try another engine, or clear your filters.

**Why does the Quotes card say "No quotes match these filters"?**
There are no quotes for the brand and sentiments you picked. Pick another brand, or clear the sentiments.

**What's the difference between a prompt topic and an attribute?**
A **prompt topic** is what the question was about, and it's set before the questions are asked. An **attribute** is something AI brings up in its answer about a brand, such as its prices or quality.
