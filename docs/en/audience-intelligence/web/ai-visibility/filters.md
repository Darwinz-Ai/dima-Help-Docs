# Filtering AI Visibility

By default, AI Visibility counts the answers to the unbranded prompts in your market, in every language, prompt topic and persona. The **Filters** let you change that, for example to only count the Arabic prompts, or only the prompts about one topic.

---

## 1. Opening the Filters

1. Open **AI Visibility** from the main sidebar.
2. Click **Filters** in the top corner of the page.

The filters panel has three tabs: **Prompts**, **Prompt topics** and **Personas**.

---

## 2. The Prompts Tab

![Screenshot of the Prompts tab with the Prompt language and Prompt type options](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/filters/prompts-tab.png)

### Prompt language

Pick **English**, **Arabic** or both. When neither is picked, prompts in all languages are counted.

### Prompt type

- **Unbranded:** prompts that don't name your brand, e.g., _Which brand is the best choice?_ These show whether AI brings you up on its own.
- **Branded:** prompts that name your brand, e.g., _What is your brand known for?_ These show what AI says about you when someone asks about you directly.

![Diagram comparing branded prompts, which name your brand, with unbranded prompts, which don't](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/filters/filters-branded-vs-unbranded.png)

> **Note:** AI Visibility starts on **Unbranded** prompts only. In branded prompts, AI almost always mentions you because the question already does, which would make your mention rate look higher than it really is.

---

## 3. The Prompt Topics Tab

![Screenshot of the Prompt topics tab with a few topics picked](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/filters/topics-tab.png)

Pick one or more **prompt topics** to only count the answers to prompts about them. When none is picked, all prompt topics are counted.

---

## 4. The Personas Tab

![Screenshot of the Personas tab with a persona picked](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/ai-visibility/filters/personas-tab.png)

Pick one or more **personas**, the types of customers the prompts are written for, to only count the answers to their prompts. When none is picked, all personas are counted.

---

## 5. Applying Your Filters

Click **Apply** to update the page. Click **Reset** to go back to the default filters, or close the panel without applying them.

Your filters apply to the **Overview**, **AI Perception** and **Trends**, except for the **Recommendations**, which always cover all your answers for the month.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why did my mention rate go up when I picked Branded prompts?**
Branded prompts already name your brand, so AI mentions you in almost every answer. Use unbranded prompts to see how visible you really are.

**Why does the page say "No data for this selection" after filtering?**
No answers match your filters for this AI engine and month. Try fewer filters, or click **Reset**.
