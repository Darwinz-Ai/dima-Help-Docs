# Adding a Company

<video controls=""><source src="https://dubble.so/media/video/01b7c4e3-7454-41da-b3e2-998b69e08706" type="video/mp4"></video>

A **Company** in dima represents the brand, competitor, or subject matter you want to monitor (e.g., _Tesla_ or _Automotive Industry in Saudi Arabia_).

Adding a company allows you to define tracking parameters like official page links, topics of interest, and Google Map locations. Once set up, dima will track relevant social media posts, allowing you to generate reports, receive alerts, and discover key insights.

To get started, click the **Add New Company** button located at the first position on the [Brands Page](https://localhost:3000/en/brands).

---

## Step 1: Company Details

![Screenshot of the Step 1 Company Details form showing input fields for Company Name, Company Description, Company Logo, Currency, Country Code, and Competitors](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/brands/add-company/add-company-step-1.png)

In this first step, you will provide the foundational information about the brand.

1. **Company Name:** Enter the official name of the company.
2. **Company Description:** Describe the company's products, services, market focus, and regions served.
   _Note: This heavily helps dima understand what content is actually relevant to the brand._
3. **Company Logo:** Click the logo box on the right to upload a recognizable image (JPEG, max 5MB).
4. **Currency & Country Code:** Use the dropdowns at the bottom left to set the primary currency and operating country.
5. **Competitors (Optional):** If this company is a competitor to your main brand, check the **"Is Competitor"** box and select the primary company it competes with from the dropdown menu.

Once filled out, click **Next** at the bottom-right corner to move to Online Presence.

## Step 2: Online Presence _(Optional)_

![Screenshot of the Step 2 Online Presence screen displaying sections for configuring Post Topics, Own Page Links, Google Map Links, and Group Links](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/brands/add-company/add-company-step-2.png)

> **Note:** Step 2 is _completely optional_. You can always skip this now and configure your company's online presence and links later from the [Manage page](https://localhost:3000/en/docs/manage) at any time.

This step tells dima exactly where to look for official posts, reviews, and targeted conversations.

### Post Topics

![Screenshot of the Post Topics configuration section showing the default General topic card and an Add Topic button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/brands/add-company/add-company-step-2-topics.png)

Categorize and track specific types of mentions (e.g., tracking complaints vs. product praise).

- Click **Add Topic**.
- Give your topic a **Name** and a brief **Description** of what it covers (e.g., _"Any feedback or complaints regarding customer service"_).
- _Note: The "General" topic is a default and cannot be removed._ It serves as a catch-all for mentions that don't fit into your custom topics.

### Own Page Links

![Screenshot of the Own Page Links section displaying form fields for Name, Platform dropdown, URL, and a checkbox to track all posts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/brands/add-company/add-company-step-2-own-pages.png)

Link the brand's official social media profiles so dima can distinguish between the brand's own posts and public mentions.

1. Click **Add Own Page**.
2. Enter a **Name** for the page and select the **Platform** (e.g., Twitter, LinkedIn) from the dropdown.
3. Paste the exact **URL** of the profile.
4. **Tracking Scope:** By default, dima tracks all posts from the last 30 days. If you want to limit tracking to specific types of posts, uncheck "Track all posts" and leave a comment specifying what dima should track.

### Google Map Links

![Screenshot of the Google Map Links section showing input fields for a location Name and its Google Maps URL](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/brands/add-company/add-company-step-2-locations.png)

Link physical locations to monitor Google Reviews.

1. Click **Add Location**.
2. Give the location a **Name** (e.g., _"Main Branch HQ"_).
3. Paste the exact **Google Maps URL**.

### Group Links

![Screenshot of the Group Links section showing fields for a Facebook group Name, URL, and tracking scope configurations](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/brands/add-company/add-company-step-2-groups.png)

Track conversations happening inside specific Facebook groups.

1. Click **Add Group** and provide a **Name**.
2. Paste the **Facebook Group URL**.
3. **Tracking Scope:** Just like Own Pages, you can choose to track all posts or specify rules (e.g., _"Only track posts mentioning drinks"_) by unchecking the tracking box and leaving a comment.

Click **Next** to move to Posts Relevancy.

## Step 3: Posts Relevancy _(Optional)_

![Screenshot of the Step 3 Posts Relevancy screen featuring two main columns to add URLs for Relevant Posts and Irrelevant Posts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/brands/add-company/add-company-step-3.png)

> **Note:** Step 3 is _also completely optional_. You can define posts relevancy criteria later in the [Monitor screen](http://localhost:3000/en/docs/audience-intelligence/monitor/deleting-posts-and-recalibrate-relevancy) if you wish to refine what dima should track or ignore for your company.

In this step, you can train dima's AI by providing examples of what you do and do not want to see. This can drastically improve the accuracy of your media monitoring, but it is not required to proceed.

- **Relevant Posts (Left Column):** Click **Add Relevant Post** and paste the URL of a post that is a perfect example of what you want dima to track.
- **Irrelevant Posts (Right Column):** Click **Add Irrelevant Post** and paste the URL of a post that dima should ignore (e.g., a post about a company with a similar name in a different industry).

You can add multiple examples for both. Once you are satisfied, click **Next**.

---

## Step 4: Review & Submit

![Screenshot of the Step 4 Review & Submit screen showing a summary of the entered company data and a Submit button at the bottom](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/brands/add-company/add-company-step-4.png)

1. Review all the configurations you have set across the previous steps.
2. Click the **Submit** button to complete the process.

> **Note:** dima will immediately begin analyzing your configuration and fetching historical data. Please allow **up to 24 hours** for the initial learning phase and data population to complete.
