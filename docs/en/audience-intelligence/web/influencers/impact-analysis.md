## 1. Viewing and Understanding Your Impact Analytics

![Screenshot of the Influencer Impact Analytics dashboard showing the overview table and various pie and bar charts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/influencer-impact-analysis/impact-dashboard.png)

To access performance analytics, simply click the **Influencer Impact Analytics** tab within the **Influencers** page in the sidebar. This opens a comprehensive dashboard displaying a variety of charts and tables that help you interpret your influencers' results.

### The Overview Table

At the top of the page, the **Influencers Overview** table lists each influencer along with their platform, views, engagement, and total post count.

### Pie Charts (Who is working)

The pie charts tell you _which specific influencer_ is performing best. A larger slice represents a higher number.

- **Post Count by Influencer:** Shows who posts the most.

- **Engagement by Influencer:** Shows who gets the most likes, comments, and shares.

- **Views by Influencer:** Shows who gets seen the most.
  > **Tip:** You can use the two icons in the top-right corner of any pie chart to switch between a piechart view and a barchart view.

### Bar Charts (Which platform is working)

The bar charts show you _which social platform_ is performing best, so you know where to focus your efforts. The colors inside each bar represent different influencers posting on that platform.

- **Post Count by Platform:** Shows how many posts went out on platforms like YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, and Instagram.

- **Engagement by Platform:** Shows which platform gets the most reactions.

- **Views by Platform:** Shows which platform generates the most views.
  > **Tip:** Use the **Display Top 10** button in the top-right corner of these charts to limit the view to your ten biggest performers.

## 2. Using the Impact Filter

![Screenshot of the Influencer Impact Analytics Filter menu showing the General and Content tabs](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/influencer-impact-analysis/impact-filters.png)

To narrow down the data shown in your dashboard, click the **Filter** button in the top-right corner of the page. The filter panel is divided into two main tabs on its left edge: **General** and **Content**.

### The General Tab

- **Sentiment:** Filter content by Positive, Negative, or Neutral sentiment tiles.

- **Emotion:** Filter by specific feelings, including Trust, Joy, Fear, Surprise, Sadness, Disgust, Anger, and Anticipation.

- **Platform:** Select one or multiple platforms, including X, Facebook, Web, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Snapchat.

### The Content Tab

- **Influencers:** Click the "Select influencers..." dropdown to search and tick specific profiles, or use "Select All".

- **Topic:** Click the "Select topics..." dropdown to filter by specific subjects (e.g., General, Smartphones, Laptops).

- **Label:** Click the "Select labels..." dropdown to filter by your custom tags.

- **Type:** Use the 2x2 grid at the bottom to select one or multiple post types, including Organic Mentions, Public Groups, Private Groups, and Own Page.

### Applying Your Filters

Once you have made your selections, click the **Apply** button in the bottom-right corner of the panel to update your dashboard.

_(Note: You can click the **Reset** button to clear your choices, or click the **X** in the top-right corner to close the panel without applying changes.)_
