# Influencer Impact Analytics

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/impact-analysis/impact-analytics.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

The **Influencer Impact Analytics** page shows how much content your matched influencers have published about your company, both **posts** and **stories**, and how far it reached. Use it to see who is delivering, compare influencers side by side, and spot which platforms and influencer sizes bring the most content.

---

## 1. Opening Impact Analytics

![Screenshot of the Influencer Impact Analytics page with the scorecards, the Influencer Performance table and the two content distribution cards](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/impact-analysis/impact-dashboard.png)

1. Open the **Influencers** page from the main sidebar.
2. Click **Influencer Impact Analytics**.

The page only includes influencers matched to your company. If it's empty, [learn how to match influencers here.](/en/guides/influencers/influencers-pool)

---

## 2. Reading the Scorecards

![Screenshot of the three scorecards: Total Views, Influencers With Content and Total Content](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/impact-analysis/scorecards.png)

The scorecards at the top sum up everything on the page:

- **Total Views:** how many times the content was seen, split into views **On posts** and **Est. story views**.
- **Influencers With Content:** how many of your matched influencers published at least one post or story in the period.
- **Total Content:** how many pieces of content were published, split into **Posts** and **Stories**.

> **Note:** Story views are **estimated**. Platforms don't share the real view count of a story, so dima estimates how many people are likely to have seen it, based on the influencer's followers and how many views their reels have been getting.

## 3. Comparing Influencers

![Screenshot of the Influencer Performance table with the Posts and Stories column groups](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/impact-analysis/influencer-performance-table.png)

The **Influencer Performance** table lists each influencer with their follower count and **Brand Relevance**, followed by two groups of columns:

- **Posts:** the posts' **Views**, **Engagement** (likes, comments and shares) and **Count**.
- **Stories:** the stories' **Est. Views** and **Count**.

Under the posts count, dima shows how many of the posts the influencer **authored** themselves and how many were a **collab**, where they were tagged as a collaborator on someone else's post.

Click an influencer's row to open their content in a new tab, where you can browse it in the **Post Monitor** and **Story Monitor**.

---

## 4. Seeing Where the Content Comes From

![Screenshot of the Content by Platform and Content by Influencer Size cards, with posts and stories shown in each bar](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/impact-analysis/content-distribution.png)

Two cards at the bottom break the content down, with **posts** and **stories** shown in different colors in each bar:

- **Content by Platform:** how much content went out on each platform, such as Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.
- **Content by Influencer Size:** how much content came from **Nano**, **Micro**, **Mid**, **Macro** and **Mega** influencers. Influencers dima hasn't sized yet are listed as **Pending**.

Each row shows how many influencers it covers and its share of all the content. Hover over a row to see its posts and stories counts and the influencers behind them. Click a row to open that content in a new tab.

## 5. Filtering the Page

![Screenshot of the Impact Analytics filter panel on the Content tab, with the posts-only note above Label and Type](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/impact-analysis/impact-filters.png)

The bar at the top of the page decides which content is counted:

- **Search:** only include content whose text or media description contains your search.
- **Date range:** only include content from a period. [Learn how to use the date filter here.](/en/guides/common/date-filter)
- **Filter:** opens the filter panel, which has two tabs on its left edge.

### The General Tab

- **Sentiment:** _Positive_, _Neutral_ or _Negative_.
- **Emotion:** _Trust_, _Joy_, _Fear_, _Surprise_, _Sadness_, _Disgust_, _Anger_ or _Anticipation_.
- **Platform:** one or more platforms, such as X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and Snapchat.

### The Content Tab

- **Influencers:** pick the influencers to include. Leave it empty to include all your matched influencers.
- **Topic:** your company's topics.
- **Label:** your custom labels.
- **Type:** _Organic Mentions_, _Public Groups_, _Private Groups_ or _Own Page_. When you pick a group or own page type, a **Groups** or **Own Pages** picker appears so you can narrow it down further.

> **Note:** **Label**, **Type**, **Groups** and **Own Pages** only apply to **posts**, not stories. Stories are still counted when these filters are set.

### Applying Your Filters

Click **Apply** to update the page. Click **Reset** to clear your choices, or the **X** in the top-right corner to close the panel without applying them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is a story's view count marked "Est."?**
Platforms don't share how many people watched a story, so dima estimates it from the influencer's followers and how many views their reels have been getting. Post views come from the platform itself.

**Why didn't the story numbers change when I filtered by Label or Type?**
Those filters only apply to posts. Use **Influencers**, **Topic**, **Sentiment**, **Emotion**, **Platform**, the search or the date range to narrow down stories.

**How do I watch an influencer's stories?**
Click their row in the Influencer Performance table, then open **Story Monitor**. [Learn how to use the Story Monitor here.](/en/guides/influencers/story-monitor)
