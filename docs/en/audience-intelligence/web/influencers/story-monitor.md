# Influencer Story Monitor

Influencers often mention your brand in **stories** as well as posts. Stories disappear after a day on social media, but dima keeps the ones about your company so you can watch them later.

The **Story Monitor** collects your matched influencers' stories about your company. You can watch the latest ones like you would on social media, or browse, sort and inspect them all.

---

## 1. Opening the Story Monitor

![Screenshot of a matched influencer card with the Content Performance button highlighted](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/story-monitor/content-performance-button.png)

1. Open the **Influencers** page from the main sidebar and go to **Matched Influencers**.
2. On the influencer you want to check, click **Content Performance**.
3. In the **Content Performance** menu, click **Story Monitor**. Click **Post Monitor** to go back to their posts, or **Reply Analytics** to see how people reacted to them.

![Screenshot of the Story Monitor with the influencer list, the Content Performance menu, the recent stories strip and the story cards labelled](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/story-monitor/story-monitor-page.png)

The list on the left shows all the influencers matched to your company. Click another influencer to see their stories instead, or use the search field at the top of the list to find one.

> **Tip:** To see the stories of several influencers together, open **[Influencer Impact Analytics](/en/guides/influencers/impact-analysis)** and click a row in the **Influencer Performance** table or one of the content distribution cards. The content opens in a new tab, where **Story Monitor** shows the stories of all the influencers in that row.

## 2. Watching Recent Stories

![Screenshot of the recent stories strip above the story cards, with an unwatched and a watched story ring](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/story-monitor/recent-stories-strip.png)

The strip at the top of the Story Monitor shows the influencers with **recent stories**: stories posted in the last 24 hours. Each story stays in the strip for 24 hours after it was posted, just like on social media. After that, you can still find it in the story cards below.

A colorful ring means there are stories you haven't watched yet. Once you've watched them, the ring turns grey.

> **Note:** dima remembers which stories you've watched in your browser. If you clear your browser's cache or site data, or switch to another browser or device, your watched stories show as unwatched again.

Click an influencer in the strip to start watching. You can also click the ring around an influencer's picture on a story card, or on the **Matched Influencers** list (hover over it to see how many stories are waiting, e.g., _Watch 3 stories_).

![Screenshot of the story player playing an influencer's video story, with the media description at the bottom and the save button at the top](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/story-monitor/story-player.png)

In the story player:

- Video stories play with sound. Photo stories stay on screen for a few seconds.
- The **media description** at the bottom explains what the story shows, so you can follow it even with the sound off.
- Hover over the story to pause it.
- Use the **Previous** and **Next** arrows to move between stories.
- Click the download button to **Save as video** (or **Save as image** for photo stories).

When there are no stories left to watch, dima lets you know you're all caught up.

> **Note:** The strip always shows the latest stories of the influencers you're looking at, whatever date range, search or other filters you've set. The cards below it follow all your filters.

## 3. Browsing All Stories

![Screenshot of the story cards in grid view, with the View settings and sort dropdowns open](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/story-monitor/story-cards.png)

Below the strip, every story in your date range is shown as a card. Each card shows:

- The influencer's picture, name, username and platform, and when the story was posted.
- The story itself.
- Its **media description**.
- Its **sentiment**, **emotion** and **topic**.
- Its **anticipated views** (the eye icon): an estimate of how many people are likely to have seen it, based on the influencer's followers and how many views their reels have been getting. Platforms don't share a story's real view count, so this gives you a realistic idea of its reach.

![Diagram showing that a story's anticipated views are estimated from the influencer's followers and how many views their reels have been getting](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/story-monitor/story-monitor-views-estimate.png)

Use the buttons above the cards to change how they're shown:

- **View settings:** switch between **Grid View** and **List View**.
- **Sort:** order the stories by **Date** or **Anticipated views**.
- **Pages:** move between pages of results when there are many stories.

## 4. Viewing a Story's Details

![Screenshot of the Story Details panel showing the story with its playback controls and full media description](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/story-monitor/story-details.png)

Click a story card to open its **Story Details** panel. There, you can play the story with its controls and read its full media description. Click the card again, or close the panel, to go back.

---

## 5. Filtering Stories

The bar at the top of the page works the same way as in the **Post Monitor**:

- **Search:** only show stories whose text or media description contains your search.
- **Date range:** only show stories from a period. [Learn how to use the date filter here.](/en/guides/common/date-filter)
- **Filter:** narrow the stories by **Sentiment**, **Emotion** and **Platform** (General tab), and by **Topic** (Content tab). Click **Apply** to update the cards.

![Screenshot of the filter panel's Content tab in the Story Monitor, with the posts-only note and the greyed-out Label and Type filters](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/story-monitor/posts-only-filters.png)

> **Note:** **Label**, **Type**, **Groups** and **Own Pages** only apply to posts, so they are greyed out in the Story Monitor. To change them, go back to the **Post Monitor**.

These filters are shared with the **Post Monitor**, so you can switch between an influencer's posts and stories without setting them up again.

![Diagram showing which filters apply to both posts and stories, which only apply to posts, and that the recent stories strip always shows the last 24 hours](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/story-monitor/story-monitor-filters.png)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does it say "No stories found"?**
None of the influencer's stories match your date range or filters. Try a wider date range, or clear the search and filters.

**Why is the recent stories strip missing?**
The influencer hasn't posted any stories in the last 24 hours. You can still find their older stories in the cards below, within your date range.

**Why are stories I already watched showing as new again?**
Watched stories are remembered in your browser. Clearing your browser's cache or site data, or using another browser or device, resets them.

**Why are a story's views called "anticipated"?**
Platforms don't share how many people watched a story, so dima estimates it from the influencer's followers and how many views their reels have been getting. Post views, on the other hand, come from the platform itself.

**Can I see the comments on a story?**
No. Stories don't have public comments, so **Reply Analytics** only covers posts. [Learn how to use Reply Analytics here.](/en/guides/influencers/comment-analytics)
