# Influencer Reply Analytics

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/comment-analysis-en.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

When an influencer posts about your brand, the comments under the post show how their audience actually reacted. **Reply Analytics** gathers the comments on an influencer's posts about your company and breaks them down by **sentiment**, **emotion**, **topic** and the **words** people use most.

You can look at the comments across many posts at once, or at a single post's comments from its details panel.

---

## 1. Opening Reply Analytics

![Screenshot of a matched influencer card with the Posts About Me button highlighted](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/posts-about-me-button.png)

1. Open the **Influencers** page from the main sidebar and go to **Matched Influencers**.
2. On the influencer you want to analyze, click **Posts About Me**. This opens the influencer's posts about your company from the last 12 months.
3. In the **Influencer Posts** menu, click **Reply Analytics**. Click **Monitor** to go back to the posts themselves.

![Screenshot of the Reply Analytics page with the influencer list, the Influencer Posts menu, the page filters, the Comment filters bar and the charts labelled](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/comment-analytics-page.png)

### Switching influencers

The list on the left shows all the influencers matched to your company. Click another influencer to see the comments on their posts instead, or use the search field at the top of the list to find one.

> **Note:** If the list is empty, no influencers are matched to your company yet. [Learn how to match influencers here.](/en/guides/influencers/influencers-pool)

> **Note:** If you switch to another company, dima keeps you on the same influencer when they are matched to the new company too. Otherwise, it switches to one of the new company's matched influencers and lets you know.

## 2. Choosing Which Posts to Analyze

![Screenshot of the page filters with the search field, the date range and the Filter button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/page-filters.png)

The filters at the top of the page decide **which posts** are included. Reply Analytics then looks at the comments on those posts.

- **Search:** only include posts whose text contains your search.
- **Date range:** only include posts from a period. It starts at the last 12 months. [Learn how to use the date filter here.](/en/guides/common/date-filter)
- **Filter:** narrow the posts by their **Sentiment**, **Emotion** and **Platform** (General tab), and by **Influencers**, **Topic**, **Label** and **Type** (Content tab). Click **Apply** to update the page.

These filters are shared with the **Monitor** view, so you can switch between your posts and their comments without setting them up again.

## 3. Filtering the Comments

![Screenshot of the Comment filters bar with the Sentiment, Emotion and Topic dropdowns and the Apply button](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/comment-filters.png)

The **Comment filters** bar above the charts filters the **comments themselves**, not the posts. Use it to focus on one kind of reaction, for example only the negative comments.

1. Pick one or more options in any of the dropdowns:
   - **Sentiment:** _Positive_, _Neutral_ or _Negative_.
   - **Emotion:** _Joy_, _Trust_, _Fear_, _Surprise_, _Sadness_, _Disgust_, _Anger_ or _Anticipation_.
   - **Topic:** your company's topics. This dropdown only appears when your company has topics.
2. Click **Apply** to update the charts. To clear the comment filters, click **Reset**.

Your comment filters stay in place when you switch to **Monitor** and back.

![Diagram showing that the page filters choose the posts, the Comment filters choose comments on those posts, and the charts show the remaining comments](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/comment-analytics-filter-levels.png)

> **Tip:** The two filters can look alike but do different jobs. Filtering **posts** by _Negative_ shows the comments on negative posts. Filtering **comments** by _Negative_ shows the negative comments, on any of the posts.

## 4. Reading the Charts

### Comment sentiment and emotion per day

![Screenshot of the Comment Sentiment per Day and Comment Emotion per Day charts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/per-day-charts.png)

- **Comment Sentiment per Day:** how many positive, neutral and negative comments came in each day.
- **Comment Emotion per Day:** how many comments showed each emotion each day.

Use them to spot the days when the reaction changed, for example a spike of negative comments after a specific post.

### Comment sentiment and emotion distribution

![Screenshot of the Comment Sentiment Distribution, Comment Emotion Distribution and the two by-topic distribution charts](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/distribution-charts.png)

- **Comment Sentiment Distribution** and **Comment Emotion Distribution:** the overall split of the comments across sentiments and emotions.
- **Comment Sentiment Distribution by Topic** and **Comment Emotion Distribution by Topic:** the same split, for the comments about one topic. Pick the topic from the chart's dropdown. It starts on your first topic.

### Comment Word Cloud

![Screenshot of the Comment Word Cloud with its Words, Hashtags, Normal, Sentiment and Emotion switches](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/word-cloud.png)

The **Comment Word Cloud** shows the words people use most in the comments. The bigger the word, the more often it appears.

- Switch between **Words** and **Hashtags** to see either the most used words or the most used hashtags.
- Switch between **Normal**, **Sentiment** and **Emotion** to color the words by the sentiment or emotion of the comments they appear in.

## 5. Seeing a Single Post's Comment Breakdown

![Screenshot of the Post Details panel showing the Comment breakdown with its Sentiment and Emotion bars and Top words](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/influencers/comment-analytics/comment-breakdown.png)

You can also check the comments on one post at a time:

1. In the **Influencer Posts** menu, click **Monitor**.
2. Click a post to open its **Post Details** panel.
3. Under the post, the **Comment breakdown** shows:
   - **Sentiment:** how the post's comments split across positive, neutral and negative.
   - **Emotion:** the post's most common emotions in its comments. When there are more than four, the rest are listed as _+N more_.
   - **Top words:** up to eight words that come up most in the post's comments.
4. Scroll down to read the comments themselves.

> **Note:** The Comment breakdown only appears when the post has comments. If a part says _Nothing to show yet_, the post's comments haven't been analyzed yet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why are the charts empty?**
There are no comments that match your filters. Try widening the date range, clearing the page filters, or clicking **Reset** on the Comment filters.

**Why don't I see the Topic dropdown in the Comment filters?**
It only appears when your company has topics set up. [Learn how to manage topics here.](/en/guides/manage/topics)

**Can I analyze the comments of several influencers at once?**
Yes. Click **Filter**, open the **Content** tab and pick several influencers under **Influencers**, then click **Apply**. If you clear the influencers there, the charts include all the influencers matched to your company.
