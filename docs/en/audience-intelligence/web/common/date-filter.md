# Using the Date Filter

<video controls=""><source src="https://dubble.so/media/video/843f17b7-6d60-4569-95c1-04664df10e12" type="video/mp4"></video>

Whenever you open an analytics page in dima, it loads with a default date range applied. The Date Filter allows you to adjust this range to find specific posts, influencers, reviews, and chart data for a given timeframe.

This filter is available across most modules, including:

- **Audience Intelligence** (Monitor, Analysis, Users, Topic Insights)
- **Groups**
- **Own Page**
- **Made For You** (When opening an already-created dashboard)
- **Conversation Analysis**
- **Influencers**
- **Locations**
- **Manage** (Deleted Posts)

---

## 1. Opening the Date Picker

To change the timeframe of the data you are viewing, click the **date-range button** located at the top of the page. This button always displays the currently applied range (e.g., "Jul 09, 2026 - Aug 09, 2026").

Clicking it will open the date picker menu, which offers several ways to select a new range.

## 2. Selecting a Date Range

You can choose your new date range using any of the following four methods:

### Quick Ranges

The left column of the date picker contains ready-made ranges for fast filtering:

- **Today / Yesterday:** Snaps the data to a single specific day.
- **Last 7 / 14 days:** Shows a rolling window of recent performance.
- **This month / Last month:** Sets the range to the exact calendar month (e.g., from the 1st up to today).

### Week of the Year

If you want to view a specific numbered week, click **Week of year** in the left column. Two dropdowns will appear below the column, allowing you to explicitly select the **Year** and the **Week Number** (e.g., Year: 2026, Week: 30).

### Calendar Selection

For a custom free-form range, interact directly with the two-month calendar in the center of the picker.

1. Click your desired **start day**.
2. Click your desired **end day**.
3. The calendar will highlight the entire period between the two dates. (You can click a new date at any time to restart the selection).

### Manual Date Entry

If you prefer to type the exact dates, use the input boxes located at the bottom of the date picker. You can directly enter the Month, Day, and Year for both the start and end dates.

---

## 3. Applying the Filter

Once you are satisfied with your selected range, click the **Set Date** button at the bottom right of the picker. The page will immediately reload, and all charts and lists will filter to match your new timeframe.

> **Note:** If you change your mind, you can click **Cancel** next to the Set Date button to close the picker without applying any changes.
