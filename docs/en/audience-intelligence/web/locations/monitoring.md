Dima extracts the google reviews of the company locations added by the user to be viewed on Dima's platform.

### [1\. Go to the sidebar \[on the leftmost part of the page\] and click on the scroll bar](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

\[the scroll bar is located next to the side bar on it's right, colored with a shade of grey\]

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/45e22f3d-e4a3-48da-a5d7-5a64bcc99213.png?0)

### [2\. Pull the scroll bar down till you see the Google Reviews tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a60baeac-5b90-43c2-893c-0b8c1aea8438/1/4.6744793653488/39.347603157775?0)

### [3\. Click on the Google Reviews icon to open the Google Reviews page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/2d656d88-364d-4a51-b8f7-432c4e1bae97/1/1.9710286209981/62.999999163902?0)

### [4\. click on the company's location you want to see people's reviews about it](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/google-reviews?h_aWQ9NjY4)

On the Google Reviews page you have the Monitor and Analysis subpages, click on the Monitor page to see all of the Company's locations with the reviews of each location shows in the star icons  
\[the tabs of the Monitor and Analytics are located under the search bar at the top of the page\]  
\[the search bar is used for looking up company's locations, it's located at the top of the page under the Dima company logo\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/062197a0-bf2c-4670-8266-ab0c26b339b8/1/7.9166665673256/28.602740144076?0)

### [5\. each location will have the same page format \[on the right\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/google-reviews?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Starts and Recent Reviews

Recent Reviews shows the reviews of different users on the Company's specific location, each review has the name of user, date of review, the review itself along with the number of stars that user rated the location with.  
You can click on the star icons to see which users rated with which amount of stars

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/6e326864-1483-4790-a9c4-1f5073179eef/1/65.191242595514/49.863015527595?0)

### [6\. Click on the Filters button to narrow down on reviews with specific criterion as shown in the step below](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/google-reviews?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[Located at the top right corner of the page under the Company's Logo\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/fa4b97ec-e86b-4dc5-a5a1-668af640a046/1/98.072918256124/10.630136777277?0)

### [7\. the filter provides 2 ranges and a list of locations to select from to see specific reviews](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/google-reviews?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[the topmost bar labelled "Filtering Range" it is used to narrow down on the range of stars the user would want to search in\], you have a drag button on the right and left of the Range bar indicating the maximum and minimum ratings the user wants to filter with (labelled with red arrows), you drag the button to the desired rating  
  
\[the bar below it labelled "Number of Reviews" is used to narrow down on the amount of review range that the user would want to search in\] ,same mechanism as Filtering Range  
  
\[the locations section at the bottom of the Filters page provide with all locations associated with the company to select from\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/09774909-bd88-48b5-acbe-cd78ad70be3e/1/0/0?0)

### [8\. The user can select from the "Locations" section the specific locations the user wants to see reviews on](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/google-reviews?h_aWQ9NjY4)

user can select one or many of the company's locations

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/8a3e3391-753f-434d-8374-738b2e1930a1/1/18.854167064031/65.287672068975?0)

### [9\. Click on Apply Filters button to narrow down on the reviews you want to see](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/google-reviews?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[located at the bottom right of the page\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/db6c7a30-4e59-4694-9f71-47911472e1e3/1/81.718751788139/85.835621454944?0)
