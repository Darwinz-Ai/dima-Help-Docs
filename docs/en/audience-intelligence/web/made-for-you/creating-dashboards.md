# Managing Dashboards

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

When you navigate to the **Made For You** module, you will land on the main screen where all of your created dashboards are housed. If your workspace is empty, you will need to build your first dashboard to get started.

---

## 1. Creating a New Dashboard

![Screenshot of the Made For You screen showing the setup form for creating a new dashboard, including fields for Name, Background Accent Color, and Assigned Companies](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/create-dashboard.png)

1. From the main Made For You screen, click the **Click to Add** button to begin building your new dashboard.
2. A setup form will appear. Fill in the required and optional details:

   - **Dashboard Name:** Enter a clear title for this workspace.
   - **Background Accent Color (Optional):** Choose a custom background color to personalize the dashboard overall.
   - **Assigned Companies:** Select the specific brands or companies you want to track within this dashboard.
   - **Company Accent Colors (Optional):** Assign a specific accent color to each selected company to easily distinguish them in your future data visuals.

3. Save your settings.

Once created, you will automatically be taken inside your new dashboard where you are ready to start adding data. To learn how to populate your workspace, please proceed to our guide on **[Creating Charts](/en/creating-charts)**.

---

## 2. Editing a Dashboard

![Screenshot showing the vertical three-dot menu on a dashboard card with the Edit, Duplicate, and Delete options](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/dashboard-actions.png)

If you ever need to rename your dashboard, adjust its colors, or change the assigned companies:

1. Navigate to the main Made For You screen where your dashboard cards are listed.
2. Locate the specific dashboard you want to update.
3. Click the **vertical three-dot menu ( ⋮ )** on that dashboard's card.
4. Select **Edit** from the dropdown menu.
5. Make your necessary adjustments and save your changes.

---

## 3. Duplicating a Dashboard

If you want to build a similar dashboard without starting from scratch, you can easily clone an existing one:

1. Click the **three-dot menu ( ⋮ )** on the dashboard card you want to copy.
2. Select **Duplicate** from the dropdown menu.
3. This action will instantly create an exact copy of the dashboard, completely retaining its selected companies, color configurations, and all the custom charts built inside of it.

---

## 4. Deleting a Dashboard

> **Warning:** Deleting a dashboard is permanent. This action will delete the dashboard and instantly remove all charts and configurations housed inside of it.

1. Locate the specific dashboard card you want to remove.
2. Click the **three-dot menu ( ⋮ )** in the top right corner of the card.
3. Select **Delete** from the dropdown menu.
4. Confirm your choice to permanently remove the dashboard from your workspace.
