# Managing Dashboards

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/new-mfy-en.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

When you navigate to the **Made For You** module, you will land on the main screen where all of your created dashboards are housed. If your workspace is empty, you can build your first dashboard to get started or choose from pre-designed templates.

---

## 1. Creating a New Dashboard

![Screenshot of the Made For You screen showing the setup form for creating a new dashboard, including fields for Name, Background Accent Color, and Assigned Companies](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/create-dashboard-step-1.png)

1. From the main Made For You screen, click the **Click to Add** button to begin building your new dashboard.
2. Fill in the initial setup form:
   - **Dashboard Name:** Enter a clear title for this workspace.
   - **Background Accent Color (Optional):** Choose a custom background color to personalize the workspace.
   - **Assigned Companies:** Select the specific brands or companies you want to track within this dashboard.
   - **Company Accent Colors (Optional):** Assign specific accent colors to selected companies to distinguish them across your data visuals.
3. Choose your dashboard starting point:
   - **Start from Scratch:** Create an empty workspace where you build and configure custom charts manually.
   - **Start from Template:** Choose from pre-configured layouts such as **Organic Content & UGC**, **Own Page Statistics**, and other specialized templates to auto-populate your workspace.

Once configured, save your settings to enter your new dashboard.

## 2. Managing & Positioning Charts

Inside your dashboard workspace, you can fully organize and control your layout:

### Drag & Reposition

Click and hold any chart header to drag and move it anywhere within the grid layout.

![Animated GIF demonstrating clicking and dragging a chart to move it to a new location on the dashboard grid](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/drag-move.gif)

---

### Resize Charts

Click and drag the edges or corners of a chart card to adjust its width and height.

![Animated GIF demonstrating pulling the edges of a chart card to resize its dimensions](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/resize.gif)

---

### Refresh Data

Click the **Refresh** button at the top of the dashboard to fetch the latest incoming data on demand.

![Animated GIF demonstrating clicking the Refresh button at the top of the dashboard to update chart metrics](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/refresh-data.gif)

_Note: Dashboards automatically fetch fresh data upon opening, but manually refreshing is useful if you leave the dashboard open for extended sessions._

---

### Saving Your Layout

After making layout updates, moving charts, or adjusting date settings, you **must click Save Dashboard** to preserve your changes.

![Animated GIF demonstrating clicking the Save Dashboard button and showing the unsaved changes prompt when navigating away](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/save-dashboard.gif)

If you attempt to navigate away with unsaved modifications, a prompt will ask whether you want to save or discard your changes before leaving.

To learn how to build individual data visuals, visit our guide on **[Creating Charts](/en/guides/made-for-you/creating-charts)**.

## 3. Dashboard-Wide Filters & Date Locking

![Screenshot showing the dashboard top filter bar, including company selections, data source filters, and date range locking options](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/create-dashboard-filters.png)

You can apply global filters across your entire dashboard to narrow down the displayed dataset without editing individual charts:

- **Global Company & Source Filters:** Use the top filter bar to include or exclude specific assigned companies or filter by data sources.
  > **Note:** For an overview of the available filters and settings for each data source, see our filter guides:
  >
  > - [Audience Intelligence](/en/guides/common/filters)
  > - [E-Commerce Reviews](/en/guides/e-commerce-reviews/filters)
  > - [Locations](/en/guides/locations/monitoring)
  > - [Conversation Analysis](/en/guides/conversation-analysis/filters)
- **Date Filter:** Define the exact timeframe for all data pulled across the dashboard.
- **Locking Date Ranges:** By default, dynamic date ranges move forward by one day each morning _(e.g., a 7-day range of Aug 17–Aug 24 today becomes Aug 18–Aug 25 tomorrow)_. Click the **Lock** icon next to the date selector to freeze the start and end dates permanently whenever you access this dashboard.
  > **Note:** After locking or adjusting your date range, make sure to click **Save Dashboard** at the top of the screen to lock that range permanently.

## 4. Exporting & Sharing

### Exporting Options

![Screenshot showing the export dropdown menu with options to download the entire dashboard or individual chart graphics and raw data](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/create-dashboard-share.png)

- **Download Full Dashboard:** Export your entire dashboard layout as an image file. You can choose whether to render **Data Label Values** directly on the visuals or export clean **Visuals-Only** graphics.
- **Download Individual Charts:** Hover over the **vertical three-dot menu ( ⋮ )** on any specific chart card, then select **Download Chart** to save it as an image or **Download CSV** to export raw data points.

### Sharing & Permissions

![Screenshot showing the share modal with options to search and invite workspace users or copy a direct shareable link](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/create-dashboard-share-dialog.png)

Click the **Share** button at the top of your dashboard to collaborate with others:

- **Invite Workspace Users:** Search and select existing team members within your account to grant them dashboard access.
  > _If you don't see a specific teammate listed, refer to our guide on **[Adding Users](/en/guides/manage/user)** to add them to your account first._
- **Shareable Link:** Generate a direct URL link to send to any verified user within your workspace.

## 5. Editing, Duplicating & Deleting

![Screenshot showing the vertical three-dot menu on a dashboard card with the Edit, Duplicate, and Delete options](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-dashboards/dashboard-actions.png)

On the main **Made For You** landing page, locate any dashboard card and click its **vertical three-dot menu ( ⋮ )** to access management options:

- **Edit:** Rename the dashboard, update assigned companies, or reassign accent colors.
- **Duplicate:** Create an instant clone of an existing dashboard, preserving all selected companies, color themes, and pre-built charts.
- **Delete:** Permanently remove the dashboard and all charts housed inside it.

> **Warning:** Deleting a dashboard is permanent and cannot be undone.
