# Creating Charts

<video controls=""><source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-charts/creating-charts-en.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

After creating a dashboard, you can start adding charts to visualize your data exactly how you want. By customizing data sources, chart types, and specific filters, you can build a highly tailored view of your brand's performance.

---

## 1. Initiating Chart Creation

![Screenshot of an empty dashboard showing the large Create chart button in the middle, and the + Create button in the top right corner](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-charts/dashboard-empty.png)

To build a new chart inside your dashboard:

1. Click the **+ Create** button located in the top right corner, just below the dashboard's name.
2. _Alternatively_, if this is your first time creating a chart in an empty dashboard, you can simply click the large **Create chart** button located directly in the middle of the screen.

---

## 2. Selecting a Data Source

![Screenshot showing the Data Source selection menu with Audience Intelligence and Locations shown](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-charts/select-source.png)

Upon clicking create, you will be prompted to select the data source your chart will pull from.

At the time of writing, supported data sources include:

- **Audience Intelligence**
- **Locations**

> **Note:** E-commerce Reviews and Conversation Analysis data sources will be coming soon in a future update.

---

## 3. Customizing Your Chart

![Screenshot of the chart creation form showing configuration fields for Chart Type, Metadata, Date Range, Value Display, Fields, and Filters](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/made-for-you/creating-charts/chart-example.png)

After picking your data source, you will be taken to the chart builder form. Configure the following sections to shape how your data is displayed:

- **Chart Type:** Choose how you want to visualize the data. Currently supported types include Bar, Stacked Bar, Line, Area, Combo, Table, Pie, Scorecard, Insights (where you can ask a question and dima responds with an answer), and Word Cloud.
- **Metadata:** Enter a clear **Title** for your chart and provide a brief **Description** (optional) for extra context.
- **Date Range:** Select the timeframe for the data. You can set it to default to the dashboard's main date range, pick fixed custom dates, or choose a rolling window (e.g., last 30 days).
- **Value Display:** Decide how data labels are shown on the chart. Choose to display numbers, percentages, both, or none at all.
- **Fields:** Select your **X-axis** and your **Y-axis** (also labeled as Metric). Depending on your X-axis selection, a **Group By** field may dynamically appear, allowing you to further categorize your data.
- **Maximum Number of Rows:** Define a row limit to prevent your chart from becoming visually cluttered. If you limit the rows, you can opt to show the excess excluded data grouped together under an "Other" category.
- **Filters:** Apply specific rules to include or exclude data based on existing filters within dima. You can filter by:
  - Assigned Companies (from your dashboard setup)
  - Sentiment & Emotion
  - Platform & Post Types
  - Topics & Labels
  - Language, Country, & Gender

---

## 4. Finalizing Your Chart

Once you are satisfied with your configurations, click the **Add Chart** button at the bottom right corner of the form. Your newly created chart will be instantly added to your dashboard!
