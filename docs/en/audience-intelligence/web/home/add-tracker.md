# Adding a Tracker

A **tracker** is how dima follows a brand, a topic or an industry on social media, for example _TheDar_, or _delivery apps in Cairo_. Once a tracker is live, dima collects the posts about it, so you can see what people are saying, get reports and spot what matters.

You only need to tell dima what you want to follow and where. dima researches it for you, finds its official pages and the different ways people spell it, and builds the tracker. You then choose what to track and check the result before it goes live. Setup usually takes about 10 minutes.

> **Note:** Only your account's main user can add trackers.

## 1. Starting a New Tracker

Click **Create** at the top of any page, next to the search.

If you don't have any trackers yet, you can also click **New tracker** on the [Home page](/en/home).

## 2. Defining What to Track

![Screenshot of the "What do you want to track?" screen with the name, kind, country and website fields](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/home/add-tracker/define.png)

On **What do you want to track?**, fill in:

1. **Brand, topic or industry:** the name people use for it, in any language, e.g., _TheDar_.
2. **What is it?** _(optional)_: pick **A brand**, **A topic** or **Something else**. Click **Add details** to tell dima more, e.g., _"The chocolate brand, not the bank with the same name"_. The more you tell it, the more precise the research. If you pick **Something else**, the details are required.
3. **Country:** the market to follow. Pick one of the countries shown, or click **Other** to search for another.
4. **Website** _(optional)_: e.g., _thedar.ai_. It helps dima find the right one.

Click **Start tracker**.

---

## 3. Waiting for the Research

![Screenshot of the Researching screen with the time left and the live list of what dima is doing](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/home/add-tracker/researching.png)

dima now researches what you asked for. The screen shows how much time is left, usually a few minutes, and what dima is doing as it goes.

You don't need to wait here. Close the window anytime and keep working: the setup carries on, and you can pick it up later, as explained in **Coming Back to a Tracker in Progress** below.

When the research is done, click **Choose what to track**.

---

## 4. Choosing What to Track

This step has three sections. Use **Next** and **Back** to move between them, or click a section in the **Sections** list on the left.

### What should we track?

![Screenshot of the "What should we track?" section with the research summary and the results, some marked Recommended](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/home/add-tracker/choose-results.png)

At the top, **We researched** sums up what dima found. If it's not what you meant, click **Not right?** to go back and change your details.

Below it, dima lists everything it found, each with the spellings it will look for. The ones marked **Recommended** are already ticked. Tick the ones you want to track, and untick the rest. You need at least one.

### Did we get it right?

![Screenshot of the "Did we get it right?" section with the facts dima found and the official pages](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/home/add-tracker/choose-findings.png)

These are the facts dima found, such as the website, products, languages, other names it's known by, management and locations. They shape the tracker, so check them:

- Click a fact to correct it. For lists, put one item per line.
- A fact you changed is marked **Edited**. Click undo to go back to what dima found.

Below the facts, **Official pages** lists the social media pages dima found. Click one to open it.

### Anything we should know?

![Screenshot of the "Anything we should know?" section with Yes, No and Not sure answers and the Anything else box](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/home/add-tracker/choose-questions.png)

When dima isn't sure about something, it asks you. Answer **Yes**, **No** or **Not sure**, and add details if you want. Answer what you can: dima follows your answers when it builds the tracker.

Use **Anything else?** for anything dima should know, e.g., _"Our official accounts, or topics to leave out."_

Click **Build tracker**.

---

## 5. Reviewing the Tracker

dima builds the tracker, which takes a few more minutes. Again, you can close the window and come back later. When it's ready, click **Review now**.

The review has two sections. Nothing goes live until you start tracking, so take the time to check them.

### Company

![Screenshot of the Company section with the logo, name, website, description, country and currency, and the official pages](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/home/add-tracker/review-company.png)

This is how the tracker appears in dima:

- **Logo:** click **Upload logo** or **Change logo** to pick an image from your device: JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 1 MB.
- **Name**, **Website** and **Description:** click any of them to change it.
- **Country and currency** are set by dima and can't be changed here.
- **Official pages:** the pages dima will collect posts from. Click a link to correct it, or click **×** to remove a page that isn't right.

### Sample posts & topics

![Screenshot of the Sample posts & topics section with the topic chips and sample posts showing why each one matched](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/home/add-tracker/review-topics.png)

- **Topics:** the topics dima will sort the posts into. Hover over a topic to see what it covers. Click **×** to remove one, or **Add topic** to add your own.
- **Sample posts:** posts dima found while building the tracker, with **Why it matched** under each one. Use them to check the tracker picks up the right posts. Click a platform to only see its posts.

> **Note:** A tracker needs exactly one **General** topic. It collects the posts that don't fit any of your other topics.

If the tracker isn't what you wanted, click **Start over** to go back to the first step.

---

## 6. Going Live

![Screenshot of the "Start tracking" confirmation dialog](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/home/add-tracker/go-live.png)

1. Click **Start tracking**.
2. In the dialog, click **Start tracking** again to confirm.

dima saves the tracker and lets you know when it's live. It starts collecting posts right away, and the tracker shows up on the [Home page](/en/home) and in the company switcher with your other trackers.

If something needs fixing first, dima takes you to the field to change.

---

## 7. Coming Back to a Tracker in Progress

![Screenshot of a tracker in progress on the Home page, and the In progress list in the notifications](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediamonitor/dimabothelp/images/en/audience-intelligence/web/home/add-tracker/in-progress.png)

Trackers you're still setting up appear in two places:

- On the **Home page**, as cards before your other trackers. Each card shows where the setup is, e.g., _Researching_, _Awaiting your choice_ or _Awaiting your review_.
- In the **notifications**, under **In progress**, with the time left. When a tracker needs you, it says **Ready to choose** or **Ready to review**.

Click a card or a notification to pick up where you left off.

### Deleting a tracker in progress

To stop setting up a tracker you no longer need, click **Delete tracker** at the bottom of the setup window, or the trash icon on its Home card. Then click **Delete** to confirm. You can't undo this.

## After Setup

Some settings aren't part of the tracker setup. Once your tracker is live, you can manage them from the **Manage** pages:

- [Own pages](/en/guides/manage/own-pages): add or change the official pages dima follows.
- [Groups](/en/guides/manage/groups): follow conversations inside Facebook groups.
- [Locations](/en/guides/manage/locations): follow Google Maps reviews of your branches.
- [Topics](/en/guides/manage/topics): change how posts are grouped.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why can't I see the Create button?**
Adding trackers isn't available on every account. On a phone, **Create** is at the bottom of the company switcher instead of the top bar.

**Why does it say "Tracker limit reached"?**
Your account has reached the number of trackers it can have. Contact support to add more.

**Why does it say "The setup didn't finish"?**
dima couldn't complete the research or the build. Click **Edit & retry**, check the name and country, then try again.

**Why does it say "Your account already has a tracker with this name"?**
Each tracker needs its own name. Change the name in the **Company** section of the review, then start tracking again.

**How long until I see posts?**
dima starts collecting posts as soon as the tracker is live. It can take a while for the first posts to show up.
