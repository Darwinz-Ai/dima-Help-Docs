dima allows users to filter posts on the Own Page, Groups and Audience Intelligence modules through several factors including: Sentiment, Emotion, Topic, Label, Platform, Language, Region and Gender.

### [1\. Click on Filter button located at the top right corner of the page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[labelled with the red arrow\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/6c9e1660-5de9-4c42-94bf-56970a93d236/1/0/0?0)

### [2\. In Filters tab you have 4 tabs to further filter with they are: Sentiment & Emotion, Topics & Labels, Platforms & Post Type, Demographic. In each of these pages fill in/select the criterion for filtering](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)


![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/32265a41-071b-4f50-9a7f-2d202950c2bb/1/24.881998697917/26.301369863014?0)



### [3\. Click on Apply button to apply the filter](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[located at the bottom right corner of the page\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/ad207ac3-d4d3-4f9c-9983-40a0c6eeee77/1/81.718751788139/85.895552961794?0)

### [4\. Click on the icon in between the Filter and Download Report buttons \[at the top right corner of the page\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[labelled with a red arrow\]

it shows the filters in a expanded fashion all of the selection criteria in one page
