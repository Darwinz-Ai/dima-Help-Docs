Dima allows users after adding posts to the collection, to be able to send reports (in several formats such as: presentation, email, table) to contacts. Simply go to the Audience Intelligence module then add posts to the collection then click on download report and select from it the type of report to send and which contacts to send it to.

### [1\. Select the company you want it's posts which are tracked by Dima to be added to a collection for downloading reports based on the posts gathered in the collection](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

\[the scroll bar to navigate with through the different companies in the brands page is via the grey scrollbar on the rightmost of the page\]

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/74a9dd76-6b1e-4485-8a65-b66d6f612e5f.png?0)

### [2\. Click on the checkbox \[located at the top right corner of the post\] to select one/many posts which will be added to the collection](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY5JnRhYj1tb25pdG9y)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/536e8fd4-85b7-4556-b1f8-9f55a34bcf5e/1/98.020021120707/37.808219178082?0)

### [3\. Click on the folder icon \[located in between the posts' statistics and the posts themselves \]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY5JnRhYj1tb25pdG9y)

then click on "Add to Collection", the "Remove from Collection" is used to remove posts from an already created collection

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/bd5fb378-71e9-4e08-b031-c44b13fa6516/1/27.239582935969/30.08219261692?0)

### [4\. Click on the "Download Report" button located at the top right corner of the page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY5JnRhYj1tb25pdG9y)

this will show a list of 3 reports to download: Presentation, Email, Table  
click on the type of report you want to download, the report will be sent by email

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/db8c0155-551d-4fd4-827b-1f35ad71b741/1/99.11458392938/15.013698421112?0)

### [5\. This will open for you a pop-up labelled "Send confirmation"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY5JnRhYj1tb25pdG9y)

in this page you can select the contacts you want to send the report to for confirmation purposes of the report

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/87116e30-8121-4b62-bf89-8f1ee0806e50/1/49.999999006589/43.726029069456?0)

### [6\. Click on the textbox/dropdown labelled "Contacts" \[located at the top of the pop-up labelled "Send confirmation"\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY5JnRhYj1tb25pdG9y)

You can either type in the desired email for narrowing down or select the email from the dropdown by clicking on the checkbox or the email itself

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/0c45ec7a-f253-426b-8119-6369f8715805/1/49.999999006589/56.876714000963?0)

### [7\. This pop-up happens when user clicks on the table option (the user will send to the contact the posts in the form of a table)](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY5JnRhYj1tb25pdG9y)

all 3 options have the same pop-up except the last option (the table option) it has an extra option/checkbox labelled "Include comments" which includes the posts' comments in the table

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/ebe8a664-2caa-4f74-b369-079f84dc705d/1/34.947915871938/58.794522220141?0)

### [8\. Click on the "Send" button \[located at the bottom right corner of the pop-up\] to send the report to the selected contact/s](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY5JnRhYj1tb25pdG9y)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/932fcffe-b724-440a-ad61-cb936e791691/1/50.000002980232/42.465753424658?0)
