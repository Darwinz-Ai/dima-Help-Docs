### [2\. Aggregated Top Posts per Hour](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

This chart shows what time of day your best-performing posts go out. The circle is a 24-hour clock, running from 00:00 at the top all the way around through the day. The further a spike stretches from the centre, the more top posts happened at that hour. Here the longest spikes point at around 18:00 and 21:00, meaning evening is when your strongest content lands.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9946fcd3-0d5f-4c14-8b02-75839abcd119/1/58.854168653488/100.41095681386?0)

### [3\. Average and Individual Progression](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

**Average Progression**

This line chart tracks your page's average performance over time. The dates run along the bottom, and the line shows whether your numbers are climbing, dropping, or staying flat month to month. A steady flat line means your performance is stable.

**Individual Progression**

Same idea, but instead of one average line you get a separate colored line for each account or page you're tracking. This lets you compare them against each other and spot which one is growing and which one is falling behind.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/fd284ee3-55f5-4f90-8276-470390484c27/1/58.854168653488/38.054792848352?0)

### [4\. Top Posts per Day of Week](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

This bar chart shows which days of the week your best posts happen. Each bar is one day, and a taller bar means more strong posts on that day. Here Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are the tallest, so midweek is your strongest window, while Monday and Friday are the weakest.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/19a880fe-d497-491c-8c87-ee5b27bc72c4/1/58.854168653488/240.95892083155?0)

### [5\. Top Engagement Volume Chart](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

This one combines two things. The shaded blue line shows your daily posting volume across the date range, and each floating bubble is a top post. A bubble's height tells you what time of day it was posted: morning, afternoon, or night, marked on the right side, and a bigger bubble means it performed better.

The three buttons in the corner switch what the chart measures: Volume shows how much was posted, Engagement shows how much reaction it got, and Engagement Sentiment colors everything by whether the reaction was positive, neutral, or negative.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/158e5284-0071-462b-9a97-acc46ddaa140/1/58.854166666667/60.876713582914?0)

### [6\. Post Sentiment and Emotion Distribution](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

**Post Sentiment Distribution**

This chart shows how the mood of your posts changed day by day. Each bar is one date, and the colors inside it split into Positive, Neutral and Negative. Because it's shown as a percentage, every bar reaches 100%. The blue line running across is your total volume for that day, so you can see whether a mood shift happened on a busy day or a quiet one.

The Volume and Engagement buttons change the basis: Volume counts the posts themselves, while Engagement weighs them by how much reaction they got.

**Post Emotion Distribution**

Exactly the same layout, but broken into specific emotions. Same Volume and Engagement toggle.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/ccd90e7c-1ea7-436b-82f0-29dc6a70d75d/1/58.854168653488/55.561644671714?0)

### [7\. Sentiment and Emotion Distribution](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

**Sentiment Distribution**

This chart gives you the overall mood of all your posts in one picture, without the day-by-day breakdown. Every post is sorted into Positive, Neutral, or Negative, and the number inside each slice is the actual count of posts, not a percentage. The two icons in the top-right corner switch between a pie view and a bar view of the same numbers.  
**Emotion Distribution**

Same idea, but broken into specific emotions. It tells you what kind of feeling your content is creating. Same pie/bar toggle in the corner.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/5ab357dc-aa50-41a2-84ea-271287af1a1f/1/58.854168653488/96.547949804019?0)

### [8\. Sentiment and Emotion Distribution by Topic](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

**Sentiment Distribution by Topic**

This is the same sentiment pie, but narrowed down to a single subject. The dropdown at the top-left lets you pick which topic you want to look at, and the chart updates to show the mood for only the posts about that topic. Useful when one subject is performing very differently from the rest. Same pie/bar toggle.

**Emotion Distribution by Topic**

The same thing for emotions: pick a topic from the dropdown and see which feelings that specific subject is generating. Same pie/bar toggle.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/d6507d04-15c5-465b-9893-3f9cc6135485/1/58.854168653488/21.041100906999?0)

### [9\. Word Cloud](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

This shows the words that appear most often across your posts. The bigger the word, the more it was used.

The Words / Hashtags tabs on the left switch between everyday words and hashtags only. The Normal / Sentiment / Emotion buttons on the right change the coloring: Normal is plain, Sentiment colors each word by whether it appeared in positive or negative posts, and Emotion colors it by the feeling attached to it. The Stop Words button lets you hide common filler words like "the" and "and" so only meaningful words remain.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/ff301d0d-3d75-48b5-a1d4-ad821099e242/1/58.854166666667/54.027398514421?0)

### [10\. Click on highlight](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

**Country Distribution**

This shows where your posts or audience are coming from, by country. Each slice is one country with its actual count. Here Saudi Arabia dominates with 228, followed by the US at 21 and the UAE at 1. Same pie/bar toggle.

**Language Distribution**

The same breakdown, but by language instead of country — Arabic at 377 and English at 27 here. It helps you confirm which language your content should be written in. Same pie/bar toggle.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/7530ea39-6661-463a-aa4c-094677c2985a/1/38.424479961395/48.383561225787?0)

### [11\. Click on 112](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

**Views Distribution by Platform**

This shows which social platform is delivering the most views. Each slice is one platform with its real view count. TikTok is carrying the bulk here with 122M, followed by Instagram at 34.6M. Same pie/bar toggle.

**Volume Distribution by Platform**

This shows where you're actually posting the most — the number of posts per platform, not the results. Facebook leads with 138 posts and X follows with 112. Read it next to the views chart and the gap becomes obvious: you may be posting heavily somewhere that isn't returning much. Same pie/bar toggle.

**Engagement Distribution by Platform**

This shows which platform gets the most reactions — likes, comments, and shares. TikTok leads at 78.8K, then Facebook at 62K. Together with the other two, these three pies answer where you post, where you're seen, and where people actually respond. Same pie/bar toggle.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/564cde6a-2b2f-4891-beb0-c3510d189515/1/58.853760361671/51.123287253184?0)

### [12\. Click on Sentiment Distribution by Platform](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/own-pages-2?h_aWQ9NjY5)

**Sentiment Distribution by Platform**

This chart shows the mood of your content on each platform side by side. Each bar is one platform — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Snapchat, TikTok, X, and YouTube — and the colours inside split into Positive (green), Neutral (orange), and Negative (pink).

Because it's shown as a percentage, every bar reaches 100%, so you're comparing the _mix_ on each platform, not how much you posted there. LinkedIn is almost entirely positive, while Snapchat is nearly all neutral.

The blue line running across is your total volume — how many posts you actually made on each platform. It's important to read the bars together with that line: a platform can look perfect but only have two posts behind it, which isn't enough to judge.

The **Volume** and **Engagement** buttons change what the chart is based on. Volume counts the posts themselves, while Engagement weighs them by how much reaction each one got — so you see the mood of what people actually engaged with, not just what you published.

**Emotion Distribution by Platform**

Exactly the same layout, but broken into specific emotions instead of just good or bad — Joy (yellow), Trust (blue), Anticipation (teal), and Sadness (dark blue). It tells you what feeling your content creates on each platform, which can differ a lot from one to another. Snapchat leans heavily on Trust here, while LinkedIn and YouTube lean toward Joy.

Same blue total-volume line, and the same Volume / Engagement toggle.
