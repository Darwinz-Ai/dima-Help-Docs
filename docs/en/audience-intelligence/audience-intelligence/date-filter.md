Filters can be used within different modules of dima, (monitoring screen in audience intelligence, monitoring screen in own pages, influencers module, made for you dashboards etc.) to allow you to filter the data shown in each module and find specific posts, influencers, reviews, charts etc. depening on the module.

*   **Audience Intelligence:** Monitor, Analysis, Users, Topic Insights
*   **Groups:** the same categories
*   **Own Page:** the same categories
*   **Made For You:** open an already-created chart to filter it
*   **Conversation Analysis**
*   **Influencers:** Influencer Impact Analysis
*   **Google Reviews**
*   **Manage:** Deleted Posts



### [2\. Click on the date-range button at the top of the page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Any analytics page loads with a default range already applied. The date filter is the button at the top of the page showing that range — everything you see is limited to it until you change it.

\[it shows the range currently applied, e.g. "Jun 28, 2026 - Jul 28, 2026"\]

Clicking it opens the date picker: a column of quick ranges on the left, a two-month calendar in the middle, and manual date boxes with a "Set Date" button along the bottom.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/fb5250fe-8e1d-4d2d-9f55-748ad2e2aee0/1/21.145834525426/8.3287670187754?0)

### [3\. Pick a quick range — click Today](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

The left column holds the ready-made ranges. Clicking "Today" snaps the whole page to just the current day. Rest of options below it work the same way.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/1c507a1f-66d7-4478-a0f4-85efaa9f853f/1/0.052083334109435/14.465753215633?0)


### [4\.When clicking Week of year](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Choose this when you want a specific numbered week rather than a free date range. Selecting it reveals two dropdowns beneath the column — a "year" and a "week number" to select from.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/28818f4f-a9d1-4e79-b351-d082ff3c285f/1/0.052083334109435/56.109589877194?0)



### [5\.You can also type an exact date instead](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[the three boxes at the bottom-left — month / day / year — let you type the start date directly instead of picking it on the calendar\]


![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/5a2f7b36-571a-42e3-8e14-7ba065befbc2/1/16.9189453125/64.613016206924?0)


### [6\. Or pick the range straight from the calendar](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Instead of typing, you can click the days on the two-month calendar. The left calendar is June 2026 and the right is July 2026; use the arrows at the top to move to other months.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/df21d124-57be-48e2-bc3a-99057f0ab3f9/1/50/49.97260524802?0)

### [7\. Click the day you want the range to start on](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click a start day in the calendar. The cell becomes the beginning of your selection.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/1e1ccb92-c6ed-4b91-a55d-2d0fe7d9c2c0/1/30.000001192093/43.845892083155?0)

### [8\. Click the day you want the range to end on](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click a later day to close the range. Everything between the two dates is highlighted as the selected period.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a2289c0a-9964-440e-b96e-68f0103b8c2c/1/30.000001192093/25.763698212088?0)



### [9\. Click Set Date to apply](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[bottom-right of the picker\] This confirms your choice and reloads the page with every chart and list filtered to the selected range. (Use **Cancel** next to it to close without changing anything.)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/880134ad-8961-473f-b004-453cbded78f7/1/40.347492694855/65.982879220623?0)
