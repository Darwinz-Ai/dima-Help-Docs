A contact is an email address and mobile phone number where you'd like to receive reports and notifications from dima. You can have multiple contacts on dima assigned to the different companies you are monitoring. You can add, edit and delete contacts from the Manage Module. When generating reports, dima will ask you which contact you want these reports to be sent to. Scheduled reports on the company also are sent to all contacts assigned to the specific company.
