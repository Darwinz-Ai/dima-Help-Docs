A topic is the category you would like dima to classify incoming posts about your company in. For example, topics can be customer service, marketing campaigns, corporate news & partnerships, and product reviews. Topics allow you to easily filter posts about your company of interest into the categories you want to track and add reports for. You have full control over the topics within each company from the Manage module.

### [1\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)
