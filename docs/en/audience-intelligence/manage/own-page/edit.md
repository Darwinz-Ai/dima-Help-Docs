dima allows users to update the own pages of companies to be changed via changing the topic's name and description. The process is by going to the Manage module and selecting the own page tab to select from the desired own page to be edited. Click on the 3 dotted button to edit the own page.

### [1\. Select the company you want to add the location to, click on the photo of the company](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

\[the scroll bar to navigate with through the different companies in the brands page is via the grey scrollbar on the rightmost of the page\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/73c0db38-5d68-43b4-96a7-903d6b6b4521/1/50/50?0)

### [2\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c796166d-9ae4-442f-9d13-06f27891a05b/1/2.1337891618411/85.643837288634?0)


### [3\. Go to the Own Page you want to edit it's details, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired Own Page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit Own Page" or "Delete Own Page", click on "Edit Own Page"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/d1b8a5c0-a9f8-4f21-9047-0e43de9111d4/1/72.656252980232/42.301372371308?0)

### [5\. Edit the required fields to be updated: Name, Platfrom, URL, "Track all posts in the groups?"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/4287e98c-0e79-43b5-b31e-9fca2d0c2136/1/33.588867386182/40.46575258856?0)


### [6\. If you don't want to track all posts on the group you want to add then click on the checkbox with the label "Track all posts in the groups?"](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/7f75e47d-76b5-4a99-8df4-b954d702335e/1/9.9479168653488/59.86301369863?0)

### [7\. Click on the textbox labelled Comment and write what type of posts you want to track from your Own Page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c2479504-5fb6-4fac-8b94-d5edc6539324/1/50.000000993411/69.671233190249?0)

### [8\. Click on the "Update Own Page" button \[located at the bottom right corner of the page\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/cf380eb3-03c6-4bc1-9b6d-65e19a57bc5f/1/77.158206204573/80.19178416631?0)
