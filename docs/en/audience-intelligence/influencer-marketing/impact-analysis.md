### [1\. Open the Influencers section](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the Influencers icon in the left sidebar (the person icon, near the bottom of the sidebar).

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9ed05893-fe8a-405f-ab5d-ca99fa22cbe0/1/1.9710286209981/60.30137153521?0)

### [2\. Click on Influencer Impact Analytics](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

In the Influencers menu (the column just to the right of the sidebar), click Influencer Impact Analytics; it's the third item, under Influencers Pool and Matched Influencers.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/1e609197-ddff-43d5-88ed-abc44267ae30/1/6.0416668653488/24.219178500241?0)

### [3\. Read the stats and charts throughout the page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)


![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a027eff3-9089-41a8-b717-bcc20c13b357/1/65.627440810204/20.27397260274?0)


### [4\. Click on Filter and select the required criterion from the Geneal and Content tabs, this is done via selecting the desired options or filling in required feilds](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

The Filter button is located at the top-right corner of the page.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/67aa78fd-b640-4135-baa7-7a9adbe0f1a1/1/99.166671435038/8.3287670187754?0)


### [5\. Click on Apply](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click "Apply" in the bottom-right corner of the panel to apply all the filters.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c0936a62-d68a-49ec-ab66-6e2db9185039/1/81.718750794729/50.739725191299?0)
