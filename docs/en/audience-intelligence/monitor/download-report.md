dima allows users to add posts ,which are tracked by dima, to a collection which can be used later on in reports generated by dima for analytical purposes. Simply go to the Audience Intelligence module and select the posts you want to add the report with and whom to send it to.  
The report has several formats including Email, Table and Presentation.  
This feature can be found in Audience Intelligence, Groups, Own Page modules.  
Before being able to download the report, first you have to select the posts and add the selected posts to the report.

### [1\. Click on the checkbox \[located at the top right corner of the post\] to select one/many posts which will downloaded in the report](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/33b3c38b-4872-43e1-9249-c195eaacbba4.png?1)

### [2\. Click on the download icon \[located centered between the posts' statistics and the posts themselves \]](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Labelled with the red arrow and circle

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9e39d6de-2712-4a06-9ca8-4f43869e5949/1/0/0?1)

### [3\. Click on the select all icon or the dropdown next to it on the right to select/deselect all posts](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/81c7aa0b-00b8-4392-89dc-b12365b44262/1/19.635417064031/25.53424782949?1)

### [4\. Click on "Add selection to Report" to download the report with all of the selected posts](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/410f1377-4c4a-43bc-98b8-17ac337868f8/1/68.441570798556/31.123287253184?1)

### [5\. Click on highlight](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/22107647-6fd0-4728-9071-9ecaf9ca76a6/1/19.635417064031/25.53424782949?1)

### [6\. Click on Add selection to Report](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/1a293d6c-6ac7-45a5-bb4e-f5adb584155f/1/65.410158038139/31.123287253184?1)

### [7\. Click on desired report template from the dropdown menu](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[located next to the "Download Report" button on the top right corner of the page under the company logo\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/fccb106d-6e93-4423-a672-eff196b74b22/1/99.11458392938/15.013698421112?1)

### [8\. the pop-up Send confirmation will appear](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

You can select from the textbox/dropdown labelled Contacts which contacts you will send the report to, type in the contact's email for narrowing down and select on desired contact.  
You can also remove the posts from the selection after sending the report via clicking on the checkmark labelled "Keep posts in collection".

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/b1cd8f47-ae92-408f-9559-8137ef096086/1/49.999999006589/30.904110425139?1)

### [9\. Click on the "Send" button located at the bottom right corner of the pop-up to send the report](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/0073226b-1667-467e-9f0f-2acb5e56352e/1/65.572917461395/33.095891247057?1)

### [10\. You can also download the report like in step 7 via clicking on the "Download Report" button](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[located at the top right corner of the screen under the company's logo\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/73e49196-640f-4cba-8dbc-b560a780ff0f/1/93.186853826046/9.6986305550353?1)

### [11\. The Download Report pop-up will appear showing all the selected posts that will appear in the report](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

You can remove a selected post by hovering over the post and going to the trash icon on the right of the post's URL and click on the trash icon.  
you can also click the "Clear All" button \[located at the bottom left corner of the pop-up\] to remove all of the selected posts.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/79674b46-7a20-4515-9b29-3c403524557a/1/77.291671435038/32.778253424658?1)

### [12\. Click on the type of the report you want to download by clicking on the option desired](https://dima-try-34652492755.us-central1.run.app/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[the options are located at the bottom right corner of the pop-up\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/3100b00a-4fec-457e-8120-a8de182f570f/1/65.004883209864/93.194354331657?1)
