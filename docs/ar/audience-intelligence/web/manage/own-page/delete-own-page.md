dima allows users to delete the company's own pages. The process is by going to the Manage module and selecting the own page tab to select the desired own page to be removed. Click on the 3 dotted button to delete the own page. Note: deleting this own page will remove it from the company's tracked pages.

### [1\. Select the company you want to add the location to, click on the photo of the company](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

\[the scroll bar to navigate with through the different companies in the brands page is via the grey scrollbar on the rightmost of the page\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/05120a9b-2e9b-4450-a534-e92ab3cc2af4/1/50/50?0)

### [2\. Click on the Manage icon to open the Manage page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c725bda1-5edb-41f7-99a2-079e07a65563/1/1.9710286209981/85.986303042059?0)


### [3\. Go to the Own Page you want to remove, then press the 3 dotted button on the top right corner of the box of the desired Own Page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

a pop-up appears with 2 options "Edit Own Page" or "Delete Own Page", click on "Delete Own Page"

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/14b2a997-99c6-49fe-9328-0bbd0ff7bafd/1/72.656252980232/42.301372371308?0)

### [4\. Click on Delete Own Page button](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c15bd668-99b0-41e6-acc0-91232cb9d0a8/1/99.427087108294/93.205482796447?0)

### [5\. Click on the Delete Button](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/manage/own-page)
