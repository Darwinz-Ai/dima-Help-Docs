Influencer module is where you can discover a pool of new influencers that your brand may want to work with, add exisitng inluencers that your brand works with, and analyze influencer performance bennchmarking each influencer to see which influencer brings the highest ROI in terms of engagement, views etc.

### [1\. Go to Influencers tab and click on the Influencers icon](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/f5c92706-a16c-4c5a-9ebb-71bb74118bbe/1/1.9710286209981/74.876717345355?0)

### [2\. Click on Matched Influencers tab \[located on the right of the sidebar\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

this will show you all of the influencers from the influencer pool which you matched your company with

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/18f55d72-6c73-474d-a241-898a9658d61a/1/6.0416668653488/16.821918226268?0)

### [3\. Click on details button to see further information regarding the influencer](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/034d8292-701a-479b-9e2e-59d92e9f6937/1/88.822431862354/30.13698630137?0)

### [4\. Now you can see the statistics of the influencer and have several features to use](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers/influencer-info/4)

These features include:

*   TikTok profile of the influencer \[labelled with the red arrow and red circle\]
    
*   Start tracking button \[located under the TikTok profile of the influencer, has a eye icon\] is used to follow this influencer so you get their posts from now on.
    
*   Click on "Show More" to read the full bio of the influencer.
    
*   Star rating \[located under the influencer's bio\] is used as a score for this influencer's Brand relevance to your company
    
*   In addition to the tabs the user can navigate through the following tabs: Overview, Content, Risk Assessment and Post Insights.  
      
    

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/55716a9b-bd6b-46c4-b848-cd8698e62602/1/0/0?0)

### [5\. Click on Content tab to see the influencer's major posts](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers/influencer-info/4)

Such as the top and lowest performing posts and the top posts by category (categories include lifestyle, entertainment & pop culture...etc.)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a605f269-d2fb-41f6-9554-f9721434f866/1/13.900553186735/44.493153193226?0)

### [6\. the Risk Assessment section](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers/influencer-info/4)

The "Risk Assessment" tab is where the platform flags anything that could affect your brand if you work with this influencer: controversies, legal issues, or public incidents tied to their name.

Each bullet explains the situation in plain language and, importantly, tells you what role the influencer played in it: whether they were the person responsible, the person affected, or someone who resolved it.

The small blue numbers in brackets after each statement are source links. Click one and it takes you to the news article or report the information came from.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/aef54f3f-4398-48b9-a04d-42f30b3ad8df/1/19.398599863052/40?0)

### [7\. Engagement Rate by Followers Per Category](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers/influencer-info/4)

This one measures reaction against follower count instead of views.

Hover any bar for the exact percentage.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/1c223f65-3e42-4434-aa06-e5e22f85e2a7/1/29.388020435969/35.506850987265?0)

### [8\. Engagement Rate by Views Per Category](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers/influencer-info/4)

This measures how many people actually reacted out of everyone who saw the content, shown as a percentage.

Hover any bar for the exact percentage.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/16b0fde7-a286-41ab-b045-f66105dd28fd/1/29.388020435969/35.506850987265?0)

### [9\. Average Engagement Per Category](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers/influencer-info/4)

This chart shows how much reaction content gets in each category, on average. Each bar is one category, and a taller bar means more likes, comments, and shares per post.

Hover over any bar and a tooltip appears with the exact number, so you don't have to read it off the scale.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/19b2fdfc-9144-4a71-85f3-2f3a2907d93b/1/29.388020435969/35.506850987265?0)

### [10\. Click on Posts About Me \[on the right of the Details button\]](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

here you will see posts related to your company posted by the influencer
