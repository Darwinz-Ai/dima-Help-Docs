### [1\. Open the Influencers section](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the **Influencers** icon in the left sidebar (the person icon, near the bottom of the sidebar).

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9ed05893-fe8a-405f-ab5d-ca99fa22cbe0/1/1.9710286209981/60.30137153521?0)

### [2\. Click on Influencer Impact Analytics](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

In the **INFLUENCERS** menu (the column just to the right of the sidebar), click **Influencer Impact Analytics** — it's the third item, under Influencers Pool and Matched Influencers.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/1e609197-ddff-43d5-88ed-abc44267ae30/1/6.0416668653488/24.219178500241?0)

### [3\. Read the stats at the top of the page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

The **Influencers Overview** table at the top lists each influencer with their platform, views, engagement, and post count. Scroll down to see the rest of the charts.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a027eff3-9089-41a8-b717-bcc20c13b357/1/65.627440810204/20.27397260274?0)

### [4\. Charts on this page — quick guide](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

**Post Count by Influencer (pie)** Who posts the most. Bigger slice = more posts. 👉 _Two icons in the chart's top-right corner switch between donut and full pie._

**Engagement by Influencer (pie)** Who gets the most likes, comments, and shares. 👉 _Same donut / pie switch in the corner._

💡 **In short:** the **pies** tell you _which influencer_ is working. The **bars** tell you _which platform_ is working.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/6ddbe1b3-5629-4b73-8b81-fd0142f64e80/1/57.239585866531/23.287671232877?0)

### [5\. Click on DISPLAY TOP 10](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

**Views by Influencer (pie)** Who gets seen the most. 👉 _Same donut / pie switch._

**Post Count by Platform (bar)** How many posts went out on each platform — YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, Instagram. Colours inside each bar are different influencers. 👉 _The **Display Top 10** button is in the top-right corner of the chart; it limits the chart to your ten biggest._

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/707db0a1-ab1a-4f02-86b0-e2bcac90774a/1/97.552083432674/20?0)

### [6\. Click on DISPLAY TOP 10](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

**Engagement by Platform (bar)** Which platform gets the most reactions. Tells you where to focus. 👉 _**Display Top 10** button, top-right of the chart._

**Views by Platform (bar)** Which platform gets you the most views. 👉 _Same **Display Top 10** button._

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/84cc0e1d-f47d-4677-a87b-67c961ba4f3c/1/56.718749304612/34.356164801611?0)

### [7\. Click on Filter](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the **Filter** button in the **top-right corner of the page** to open the Filters panel. The panel has two tabs down its left edge — **General** and **Content**.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/67aa78fd-b640-4135-baa7-7a9adbe0f1a1/1/99.166671435038/8.3287670187754?0)

### [8\. Filter by sentiment — Positive 👍](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

On the **General** tab, the **Sentiment** row is at the top. Click **👍 Positive** (the left-most of the three tiles: Positive, Negative, Neutral).

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/047ec87f-8ec9-4e6c-ba62-eeb36be3af43/1/24.916177988052/26.301369863014?0)

### [9\. Filter by emotion — Trust 🤩](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Just below Sentiment is the **Emotion** grid. Click **🤩 Trust** (top row, middle tile — next to Joy and Fear). Other emotions below: Surprise, Sadness, Disgust, Anger, Anticipation.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/8e53735a-19fc-4553-8a73-3c46514a4676/1/53.317058086395/48.821918539805?0)

### [10\. Filter by platform — X](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Scroll down within the panel to the **Platform** grid and click **X** (top-left tile). The full set is X, Facebook, Web, Instagram, Tiktok, Youtube, LinkedIn, and Snapchat.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/f755d1f1-3a5d-478e-b853-2268faf1c97a/1/24.916177988052/46.630137717887?0)

### [11\. Switch to the Content tab](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click **Content** — the second tab on the left edge of the panel, just under **General**. This tab filters by influencer, topic, label, and post type.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/00fbd4f4-b88f-4b66-b38c-7a500fdea966/1/16.718751192093/18.630136986301?0)

### [12\. Open the influencers dropdown](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click **Select influencers…** — the first dropdown at the top of the Content tab.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/ab598525-4070-449f-8c14-7f9f06fad7fa/1/26.009929180145/23.616439610311?0)

### [13\. Pick an influencer](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Tick the influencer you want in the list — here **@ahmedhelmy**. Use the search box at the top of the list, or **Select All**, to choose several.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/8dffa37b-54a6-4d4e-afc2-9d4208f514ad/1/24.947917461395/38.136985465272?0)

### [14\. Choose a topic](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Open the **Topic** dropdown (the **Select topics…** field, second in the Content tab) and tick the topics to filter by — e.g. General, Smartphones, Laptops.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/24d6d10a-7fba-4e11-aedc-1eacc4963a2d/1/53.31746339798/41.424656698149?0)

### [15\. Choose a label](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Open the **Label** dropdown (the **Select labels…** field, third in the Content tab) and tick a label — here **test label**.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/bce48133-e842-489a-b44f-c07e05792553/1/53.31746339798/57.863012862532?0)

### [16\. Choose a post type — Public Groups](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

At the bottom of the Content tab is the **Type** section — a 2×2 grid: Organic Mentions, Public Groups, Private Groups, Own Page. Click **Public Groups** (top-right cell).

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/165f6b42-08b1-4308-853d-befbf1bbb764/1/81.718750794729/66.630136150203?0)

### [17\. Confirm the post type](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

**Public Groups** is now selected in the Type grid (top-right cell). You can select more than one type.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a457b9d5-dc37-48ae-b181-358799f5af3b/1/81.718750794729/50.739725191299?0)

### [18\. Click on Apply](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/influencers-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click **Apply** in the **bottom-right corner of the panel** to apply all the filters. (**Reset**, just to its left, clears them; the **X** in the panel's top-right corner closes without applying.)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c0936a62-d68a-49ec-ab66-6e2db9185039/1/81.718750794729/50.739725191299?0)
