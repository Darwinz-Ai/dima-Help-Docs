The made for you module allows you to add custom dashboards and reports with different charts (pie chart, bar chart etc.) and insights (dima AI generated insights like what are the top 3 causes of negative sentiment etc.) for the companies being monitored in your account. It can be used for competitive benchmarking, campaign performance and much more. All you need to do is select which companies you want to add to each dashboard you add, and add the charts and insights you want within each dashboard and filtering on the posts you want to include/exclude within each chart/insights box you add. These made for you dashboards can then be downloaded into custom reports or shared with users you add on dima as live dashboard links.



### [1\. Open dima](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

Start on the Brands page, where all of your companies are listed.

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/55246797-9b6d-43b0-8648-82a0206a8cc7.png?0)

### [2\. Click the Made for You icon in the sidebar](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

\[on the far left of the page\] This opens your custom dashboards area, where you build your own charts.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/bf06faf7-1c95-4f69-93ad-5f2301e073d3/1/6.8229168653488/14.90411000709?0)

### [4\. Click on company which this dashboard will cover](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/custom-dashboards/add)


![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/93e8650e-8138-415c-bdcd-24a7292f1d40/1/11.354167262713/46.082190944724?0)



### [5\. Fill in name of dashboard](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/custom-dashboards/add)

\[bottom-right corner of the page\]

### [7\. Click on Add Your Own Chart](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/custom-dashboards/view/392)


![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/d49db0cf-f706-4bf4-8ef7-fe2b01ad21f1/1/31.130371491114/8.3287670187754?0)

### [8\. Select desired chart](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/custom-dashboards/view/392)

### [9\. Fill in required and optional feilds: Chart Ttile, Chart Description, X-Axis, Y-Axis, Show Values, Sentiment, Emotion, Platform, Company, Topic, Label, Post Type](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/custom-dashboards/view/392)

### [10\. Click on Add Chart](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/custom-dashboards/view/392)

Click **Add Chart** (bottom-right corner of the builder) to place the finished chart onto your dashboard.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a844d8b7-a07c-4c2e-9e38-779b3c73d777/1/95.885414878527/88.596751591931?0)

### [11\. Your chart is now on the dashboard](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/custom-dashboards/view/392)

The new chart appears on the dashboard.

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/3c20c279-1003-4c72-9663-6876a606874c.png?0)

### [12\. Click on Save Dashboard (or Reset)](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/custom-dashboards/view/392)

Click "Save Dashboard" (top-right of the dashboard page) to keep your changes, or "Reset" to remove the chart (if it isn't saved yet). If you press Reset after charts are already saved, nothing changes.
