Deleting Posts and recalibrating relevancy
==========================================

*   Created by Hussein El Rayes on 7/27/2026

<video controls=""><source src="https://dubble.so/media/video/196a5a7f-0d56-4679-80cb-0868f9a19f59" type="video/mp4"></video>

### [1\. Go to the posts view](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Open the Monitor page under Audience Intelligence, where every tracked post is listed as a card.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/22779f52-19c7-44dc-8f17-cfdf6c6338f4/1/43.29833984375/37.808219178082?0)

### [2\. Select the posts you want to delete](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the checkbox at the top-right corner of a post card to select it. You can tick as many posts as you like this way.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/0b0c0831-539e-4fd9-aa3d-dab0bbccf1b2/1/71.180017789205/37.808219178082?0)

### [3\. Select at least 5 posts to recalibrate relevancy](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Selecting at least 5 posts lets dima re-learn what's relevant from your cleanup. You can also open the recalibration directly by clicking the **Recalibrate relevancy** icon in the toolbar above the posts.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/c4cc5922-e943-4ed7-bc3f-70fafb1fd25b/1/23.628471295039/26.867581720222?0)

### [4\. Click on Delete](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the **Delete** (trash) icon in the toolbar above the posts (to the right of the select-all checkbox) to remove the selected posts.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a63cd566-0e61-491d-b10b-6518ec92f4db/1/70.260418951511/59.375?0)

### [5\. The "Recalibrate Relevancy?" dialog opens](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

After removing posts, this dialog appears so you can tell dima how relevancy should be adjusted, so similar irrelevant posts stop showing up. \[the X in the top-right corner closes it without saving\]

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/17b736bb-9ed6-4a6e-8f6f-82b415078a3d/1/27.838542064031/25.863014116679?0)

### [6\. You can also reopen it from the toolbar](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

At any time you can reopen this window using the **Recalibrate relevancy** icon in the toolbar (hovering it shows the "Recalibrate relevancy" tooltip).

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/7f73869f-41e8-4e97-8bc5-9f7519d62b4d/1/90.572921435038/7.298801369863?0)

### [7\. Click on Add Irrelevant Post](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Under **Irrelevant Posts** on the right, click **Add Irrelevant Post** to add a link to a post you consider unrelated to your brand. (This is optional.)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/b0ad6934-83a5-4bcc-87cf-e47c2f34f713/1/90.052084128062/59.408389574861?0)

### [8\. Click the new link box](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the empty link box that appears at the bottom of the Irrelevant Posts list.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/607c04b5-263b-4aed-ae61-1dcd8a28b6a1/1/86.25569542249/58.860444369381?0)

### [9\. Type the link of the irrelevant post](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Paste or type the URL of the post you consider irrelevant into the box.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/2594a8c5-ee84-48e9-8f96-b86cfaaa8a3f/1/86.25569542249/58.860444369381?0)

### [10\. Or describe it in the Requested Relevancy Adjustment box](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Instead of (or in addition to) links, you can use the **Requested Relevancy Adjustment** box at the bottom to describe in plain words what should or shouldn't count as relevant for this company.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/7460de9b-bcfc-4d8d-b843-aa8a49f9b535/1/50.000000993411/90.860445205479?0)

### [11\. Remove a link you added by mistake](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the trash icon next to any link to delete it from the list.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/547523a5-94ee-4e24-88fa-2e04aedb6225/1/88.541671385368/58.860444369381?0)

### [12\. Type your adjustment request](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

In the Requested Relevancy Adjustment box, describe what you want — for example, "i want posts about laptops only".

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/2dc7942c-3361-4168-a3ec-e7dbc65036f3/1/50.000000993411/79.90154109589?0)

### [13\. Click on Submit request](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click **Submit request** at the bottom-right to send your feedback to dima. (Use **Not now** to skip.)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/86857909-e541-4d0b-83e3-38e33b18e8fe/1/90.052088101705/92.646409126177?0)

### [14\. Confirmation](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

dima confirms with "Request received — dima is learning from your feedback. Please allow up to 24 hours for the adjustment." Click the **X** in the top-right corner of the dialog to close.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/4b30146c-531d-4139-b54d-ccf11f2c6bfa/1/90.312504768372/14.383561643836?0)
