Filters can be used within different modules of dima, (monitoring screen in audience intelligence, monitoring screen in own pages, influencers module, made for you dashboards etc.) to allow you to filter the data shown in each module and find specific posts, influencers, reviews, charts etc. depening on the module.

*   **Audience Intelligence:** Monitor, Analysis, Users, Topic Insights
*   **Groups:** the same categories
*   **Own Page:** the same categories
*   **Made For You:** open an already-created chart to filter it
*   **Conversation Analysis**
*   **Influencers:** Influencer Impact Analysis
*   **Google Reviews**
*   **Manage:** Deleted Posts

### [1\. Open dima](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/brands)

Any analytics page loads with a default range already applied. The date filter is the button at the top of the page showing that range — everything you see is limited to it until you change it.

![](https://dubble-prod-01.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/3037976e-f2ff-4a76-bba4-d27e380e516c.png?0)

### [2\. Click on the date-range button at the top of the page](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[it shows the range currently applied, e.g. "Jun 28, 2026 - Jul 28, 2026"\]

Clicking it opens the date picker: a column of quick ranges on the left, a two-month calendar in the middle, and manual date boxes with a **Set Date** button along the bottom.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/fb5250fe-8e1d-4d2d-9f55-748ad2e2aee0/1/21.145834525426/8.3287670187754?0)

### [3\. Pick a quick range — click Today](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

The left column holds the ready-made ranges. Clicking **Today** snaps the whole page to just the current day. **Last 7 days** and **Last 14 days** below it work the same way.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/1c507a1f-66d7-4478-a0f4-85efaa9f853f/1/0.052083334109435/14.465753215633?0)

### [4\. Click Yesterday](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Still in the quick-ranges column on the left, **Yesterday** sets the range to the previous day only — handy for checking how a single past day performed.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/3e621cd2-a285-406f-bccc-7eb2d8be9836/1/0.052083334109435/20.49315152103?0)

### [5\. Click This month](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Lower in the same left column, **This month** sets the range to the current calendar month, from the 1st up to today. **Last month** just below does the same for the previous month.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/2d416ecd-3ffc-4c07-9ad0-73cc5c1f481e/1/0.052083334109435/38.575343301851?0)

### [6\. Click Week of year](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Choose this when you want a specific numbered week rather than a free date range. Selecting it reveals two dropdowns beneath the column — a **year** and a **week number**.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/28818f4f-a9d1-4e79-b351-d082ff3c285f/1/0.052083334109435/56.109589877194?0)

### [7\. Choose the year (2026)](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the year dropdown (bottom-left, showing **2026**) and pick the year whose week you want to view.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/5d6dc6e4-e302-463a-828e-8f1220246a4a/1/0.9895833209157/22.246576596613?0)

### [8\. Choose the week (Week 30)](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the week dropdown (showing **Week 30**) and pick the week number. The calendar highlights the matching week — here Jul 19–25.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/fe75c026-107d-48e8-b790-0d02802ef931/1/0.9895833209157/52.164386069938?0)

### [9\. Type an exact date instead — click the year box of the start date](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[the three boxes at the bottom-left — month / day / year — let you type the start date directly instead of picking it on the calendar\]

Start by clicking the **year** box ("2026") and typing the year you want.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/5a2f7b36-571a-42e3-8e14-7ba065befbc2/1/16.9189453125/64.613016206924?0)

### [10\. Click the day box of the start date](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[the middle box, "19"\] Type the day of the month you want the range to start on.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/6e290480-89ba-4f73-a3eb-4c822c182326/1/14.891764521599/64.613016206924?0)

### [11\. Click the month box of the start date](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[the left box, "7"\] Type the start month. The three boxes on the right of the arrow set the **end** date in exactly the same way.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/3f059b55-5e1e-41a3-8b0c-af651fcaa194/1/13.125000397364/64.613016206924?0)

### [12\. Or pick the range straight from the calendar](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Instead of typing, you can click the days on the two-month calendar. The left calendar is June 2026 and the right is July 2026; use the arrows at the top to move to other months.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/df21d124-57be-48e2-bc3a-99057f0ab3f9/1/50/49.97260524802?0)

### [13\. Click the day you want the range to start on](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click a start day in the calendar (here the **19th**). The cell becomes the beginning of your selection.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/1e1ccb92-c6ed-4b91-a55d-2d0fe7d9c2c0/1/30.000001192093/43.845892083155?0)

### [14\. Click the day you want the range to end on](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click a later day (here the **28th**) to close the range. Everything between the two dates is highlighted as the selected period.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/a2289c0a-9964-440e-b96e-68f0103b8c2c/1/30.000001192093/25.763698212088?0)

### [15\. Adjust the start day if needed](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Clicking another day restarts the selection from there — here the **4th** — so you can fine-tune the range as many times as you like.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/4826e7a0-2062-4350-b730-b472aad99a4c/1/44.062499205271/25.763698212088?0)

### [16\. Confirm the start day](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

The **4th** is now set as the start of the range.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/d8ed72f8-0153-4fd5-ab26-80c095d08eb2/1/44.062499205271/25.763698212088?0)

### [17\. Click a new end day](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Click the day you want to finish on (here the **21st**) to update the end of the range.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/120278eb-773a-4f03-a0b1-5084af7c61bf/1/34.687499205271/43.845892083155?0)

### [18\. Confirm the end day](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

The **21st** is set as the end of the range.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/1407a5e5-904e-4ef1-9e97-4198ec368466/1/34.687499205271/43.845892083155?0)

### [19\. Change the start day again](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

Clicking the **7th** moves the start of the range to that day.

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/ab0113d4-6812-4957-bb06-dc7bc29e9113/1/34.687499205271/31.791095472362?0)

### [20\. Confirm the final range](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

The range now reads **Jul 7 – Jul 21, 2026**, matching the boxes at the bottom (7 / 7 / 2026 → 7 / 21 / 2026).

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/9151f54d-c9b6-444a-afd8-b20fa178f9b8/1/34.687499205271/31.791095472362?0)

### [21\. Click Set Date to apply](https://dimaapp.darwinz.ai/en/audience-intelligence-2?h_aWQ9NjY4)

\[bottom-right of the picker\] This confirms your choice and reloads the page with every chart and list filtered to the selected range. (Use **Cancel** next to it to close without changing anything.)

![](https://d3q7ie80jbiqey.cloudfront.net/media/image/zoom/880134ad-8961-473f-b004-453cbded78f7/1/40.347492694855/65.982879220623?0)
